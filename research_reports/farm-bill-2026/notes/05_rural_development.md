# Title VI — Rural Development — Notes

## Title-level summary
Title VI reauthorizes USDA Rural Development programs through FY2031 (most prior authorizations ran through FY2023), with the bulk of dollar authorizations shifted to "fiscal years 2027 through 2031." Its signature reform is a wholesale rewrite of the rural broadband program (Sec. 6201), renaming it the **ReConnect Rural Broadband Program**, folding the appropriations-rider ReConnect pilot into the permanent Sec. 601 RE Act authority at **$350M/yr**, and raising the baseline broadband definition from 25/3 Mbps to **50/25 Mbps**. The title is organized into five subtitles: A (rural health — telemedicine/community facilities), B (broadband), C (miscellaneous — energy savings, precision agriculture, food supply chain, meat processing, childcare, innovation center), D (extensive amendments to the Consolidated Farm and Rural Development Act covering water/wastewater, business, and rural health facilities), and E (Rural Electrification Act). New freestanding authorities include meat/poultry processing grants, the Expanding Childcare in Rural America Initiative, the Rural Development Innovation Center, and a Rural Health Care Facility Technical Assistance Program. Numerous "anti-overbuild," affordability, and program-integrity (mapping/validation) provisions are inserted into the broadband programs. Several program-integrity guardrails (fee caps of 3% initial / 0.75% periodic, 30-day disclosure of fee changes) are added across guaranteed-loan programs.

## Section-by-section

- **Amends:** Section 6101(a) of the Agriculture Improvement Act of 2018 (132 Stat. 4726; P.L. 115-334)
- **Numbers:** —
- **Dates:** "2025" → "2027" (two places)
- **Type:** EXTEND / REFORM
- **Affected:** Rural health facilities (mental/behavioral/maternal health providers now eligible for priority); telemedicine applicants.
- **Flag:** Expands a substance-use-only carve-out into a broad behavioral/maternal-health priority.

### Sec. 6201 — Rural broadband program loans and grants (MAJOR REWRITE — ReConnect)
- **Does:** Renames the program from "Access to broadband telecommunications services in rural areas" to the **ReConnect Rural Broadband Program**. Directs the Secretary to establish a program providing grants, loans, and loan guarantees to finance broadband facilities/equipment in rural areas. Sets priority tiers: (1) **highest priority** to unserved rural communities lacking residential broadband of at least 25 Mbps down / 3 Mbps up; (2) next priority to communities <10,000 population or in geographically underserved/distressed areas (socially vulnerable, persistent poverty county, economically distressed), multi-stakeholder projects (governments, nonprofits, community anchor institutions, private/philanthropic/cooperative entities), or experienced providers (≥5 years). Requires affordability to be considered when determining "unserved." Adds grant agreement of **not less than 10 years**. Establishes grant-only and combined (single decision) application processes. Sets loan fees: **initial guarantee fee ≤3%** of guaranteed principal; **periodic retention fee ≤0.75%** of outstanding principal; 30-day public disclosure before fee changes. Expands eligible entities (states/locals, territories, Indian Tribes, co-ops/mutuals, intermunicipal organizations, corporations, LLC/LLPs) and excludes individuals/general partnerships of individuals; permits funding affiliate-owned/operated networks. Raises the build-out application threshold from "not less than 50" to "not less than 75" (percent of unserved households requirement). Raises the **minimum broadband service definition from 25/3 to 50/25 Mbps** (downstream 25→50, upstream 3→25). Allows the Secretary to raise the minimum service level by up to 50% per year (via 30-day notice/comment) if <95% of funds were obligated in the prior 2 rounds. Sets tiered buildout standards by award term: term <8 yrs = 2× minimum speed; 8–<14 yrs = 5× minimum; ≥14 yrs = 10× minimum. Authorizes appropriations and sunsets/transitions the old pilot.
- **Amends:** Section 601 of the Rural Electrification Act of 1936 (7 U.S.C. 950bb); sunsets authorities under Sec. 779 of the Consolidated Appropriations Act, 2018 (P.L. 115-141)
- **Numbers:** Authorization **$350,000,000 for each of FY2027–2031**, available until expended. Priority threshold 25/3 Mbps; minimum service raised to 50/25 Mbps; initial guarantee fee ≤3%; periodic retention fee ≤0.75%; ≥75 (from 50); ≥10-year grant agreement; ≤50% annual increase to minimum if <95% obligated; build standards 2×/5×/10×; population <10,000; ≥5-year provider experience; transition admin costs ≤1% of unobligated pilot balances.
- **Dates:** "2023" → "2031" (subsection (k)); rules within **270 days** of enactment; biennial review required; pilot sunset 270 days after enactment; consolidation of pilot funds unobligated on/before June 30, 2025.
- **Type:** REFORM / INCREASE (renamed, permanent $350M/yr authorization, higher speed standards)
- **Affected:** Winners — rural ISPs/co-ops, Tribes, unserved rural households, applicants for grant-only/combined awards; community anchor institutions. Losers — providers in already-funded/overbuilt areas; the old ReConnect appropriations pilot (sunset and merged). Secretary must promulgate rules within 270 days.
- **Flag:** Notable — converts the appropriations-rider ReConnect pilot into permanent statutory authority; substantially raises broadband speed definitions; folds/merges pilot balances; affordability now a factor in "unserved" determinations.

### Sec. 6203 — Innovative broadband advancement program
- **Does:** Fully rewrites Sec. 603 to establish the **Innovative Broadband Advancement Program**, providing grants/loans to demonstrate innovative broadband technologies that lower deployment cost and deliver substantially faster speeds in rural areas. Includes (b) terrestrial broadband demonstration projects (must reach at least the maximum project agreement requirements under 601(e)(4); 5-year build-out; priority to multi-entity partnerships and projects serving the most rural entities, replicable, geographically diverse) and (c) satellite broadband demonstration projects (reduce/eliminate cost of satellite broadband equipment for qualified consumers at/below poverty line or essential community facilities in "remote" — ERS frontier/remote ZIP levels 3–4; latency ≤250 ms; cost reduction within 1 year; annual eligibility map). Requires annual report to Ag Committees.
- **Amends:** Section 603 of the Rural Electrification Act of 1936 (7 U.S.C. 950bb-2) — rewritten
- **Numbers:** Authorization **$10,000,000 for each of FY2027–2031**; satellite latency ≤250 ms; 25/3 Mbps eligibility-map threshold; 5-year build-out (terrestrial); 1-year cost reduction (satellite).
- **Dates:** FY2027–2031; eligibility map within 1 year of enactment and annually; report within 1 year and annually.
- **Type:** NEW/REFORM (rewrites existing Sec. 603)
- **Affected:** Innovative/terrestrial broadband providers; **satellite broadband providers** (e.g., LEO services) serving remote low-income consumers.
- **Flag:** Notable — explicit new satellite broadband subsidy track for remote/poverty-line consumers; 250 ms latency ceiling.

### Sec. 6206 — Public notice, assessments, technical assistance, and reporting requirements
- **Does:** Amends Sec. 701 heading to add "technical assistance." Requires complete shapefile map in service-territory submissions. Adds a validation step for provider-submitted information (agency determination, submitter response, final non-appealable Secretary determination). Requires eligibility assessment using the FCC map (sec. 802(c)(1)(A) of Communications Act) and the Deployment Locations Map (sec. 60104(b) of IIJA). Replaces subsection (e) with a **Broadband Technical Assistance Program** (grants to private/nonprofit/public orgs to help rural entities apply for and manage broadband programs; eligible entities include Tribes, governments, higher-ed, 501(c)(3)s, co-ops, corporations, LLCs; priority to experienced providers; national/multi-state applications allowed). Adds (f) **Assistance for community broadband mapping** (grants ≤$50,000 to collect broadband data; FCC-standard accuracy; data shared with FCC/NTIA; ≤1% of subsec. (g) funds). Adds (g) set-aside: **not less than 3 but not more than 5 percent** of Title VI appropriations for oversight/accountability/this section.
- **Amends:** Section 701 of the Rural Electrification Act of 1936 (7 U.S.C. 950cc)
- **Numbers:** Mapping grant cap $50,000; ≤1% of (g) funds for mapping subsection; 3–5% set-aside for oversight.
- **Dates:** Funds reference FY2027–2031.
- **Type:** NEW / REFORM (program integrity, technical assistance, mapping)
- **Affected:** Broadband applicants (more verification); technical-assistance and mapping grantees; FCC/NTIA (data recipients).
- **Flag:** Notable — adds a non-appealable Secretary validation determination over provider-submitted coverage data; integrates FCC/IIJA maps for eligibility.

### Sec. 6207 — Limitation on overbuilding
- **Does:** Adds new Sec. 608: an area is not "unserved" if an applicant in another Federal or State broadband program has received a funding obligation to offer retail service in the area within the prior 5 years at ≥100 Mbps down / 20 Mbps up.
- **Amends:** Title VI of the Rural Electrification Act of 1936 (adds Sec. 608; 7 U.S.C. 950bb et seq.)
- **Numbers:** 100/20 Mbps threshold; 5-year lookback.
- **Dates:** —
- **Type:** NEW (anti-overbuild limitation)
- **Affected:** Excludes areas with recent other-program funding from ReConnect eligibility; protects incumbent funded providers.
- **Flag:** Notable — cross-program anti-overbuild guardrail referencing 100/20 Mbps.

### Sec. 6301 — Rural energy savings program
- **Does:** Expands eligible entities to include Indian Tribes and innovative-financing public/quasi-public/nonprofit entities; requires entities to continue serving rural areas. Redefines "energy efficiency measures" to include cost-effective on/off-grid renewables, energy storage, and replacement of manufactured housing units/large appliances. Adds **grants** alongside loans: at the election of a loan recipient, the Secretary may provide a grant of **≤5%** of the loan (or **10%** for persistent poverty counties). Adds prioritization for entities serving ≥80% rural ratepayers. Sets loan terms (≤20 years; advances amortized ≤10 years, with extension authority). Limits: special advances within first 10 years; ≤10% of annual budget authority for manufactured-housing/appliance replacement. Allows loans to any qualified consumer in the service territory regardless of rural status; adds outreach; sets 3–5% of appropriations for outreach/training/TA. Reauthorizes.
- **Amends:** Section 6407 of the Farm Security and Rural Investment Act of 2002 (7 U.S.C. 8107a)
- **Numbers:** Grants ≤5% (≤10% persistent poverty); ≥80% rural ratepayer priority; loan term ≤20 yrs; advance amortization ≤10 yrs; special advances first 10 yrs; ≤10% budget authority for housing/appliance replacement; 3–5% for outreach/TA.
- **Dates:** "2014 through 2023" → "2027 through 2031"
- **Type:** REFORM / INCREASE / EXTEND
- **Affected:** Rural electric co-ops/utilities, Tribes, qualified consumers (including non-rural within service territory); persistent poverty counties favored.
- **Flag:** Adds grant component (new spending mechanism) to a previously loan-only program; manufactured housing replacement now eligible.

### Sec. 6303 — Food supply chain guaranteed loans

## Cross-cutting flags for this title
- **Reauthorization horizon:** Most dollar programs shift to FY2027–2031; a handful use other windows — Sec. 6303 food supply chain reservation is FY2025–2029, and Sec. 6305 childcare priority is FY2027–2029. Most sunset-style extensions move single-year dates to "2031."
- **ReConnect / broadband overhaul (Subtitle B):** Permanent statutory ReConnect program at $350M/yr (6201); minimum broadband definition raised 25/3 → 50/25 Mbps; tiered buildout standards (2×/5×/10× by award term); affordability now factors into "unserved"; anti-overbuild guardrail at 100/20 Mbps (6207); new satellite broadband subsidy track with 250 ms latency cap (6203); mandatory shapefile maps and a non-appealable USDA validation determination over provider coverage data (6206); integration of FCC/IIJA maps; rate-regulation prohibition (6205).
- **Program-integrity/fee standardization:** Identical fee-cap structure (initial guarantee ≤3%, periodic retention ≤0.75%, 30-day disclosure) appears in both broadband (6201) and CFRDA guaranteed-loan programs (6412), suggesting a deliberate cross-program template.
- **Rural health emphasis:** Mental/behavioral/maternal health priorities (6101); Rural Health Liaison coordination with farm-stress network (6308); major rural health care facility refinancing + new TA program with $2M/yr (6417); health care services reauth (6423).
- **Water/wastewater package (Subtitle D):** Circuit rider rewrite with cybersecurity TA and appropriations-lapse continuity ($25M/yr, 6402); new 0%/1% distressed-water-system loans with forgiveness/refinancing (6403); decentralized water systems rewrite ($20M/yr, 6407); disaster/recovery assistance added (6429); plus multiple straight reauthorizations (6401, 6404–6406).
- **New freestanding programs:** Meat/poultry processing & rendering grants ($3M/yr, 6304); Expanding Childcare in Rural America Initiative (6305); Rural Development Innovation Center (6307); TA for geographically underserved/distressed areas (6306).
- **National-security / supply-chain framing:** Precision agriculture standards with "foreign adversary"/"trusted supplier" screening (6302); food supply chain resilience guaranteed loans up to $40M, including non-rural facilities (6303).
- **Equity targeting:** Repeated priority language for socially vulnerable communities, persistent poverty counties, economically distressed areas, colonias, and Tribal areas (6201, 6206, 6301, 6306, 6403, 6411, 6422). Persistent poverty counties get enhanced grant/match percentages (6301: 10% vs 5%; 6422: 5% match).
- **New eligibility expansions:** Indian Tribes added to several programs (6301 rural energy savings, 6409 solid waste); veterans via "Armed to Farm Initiative" with $1.5M/yr earmark (6414); satellite providers (6203); broad health-facility types (6417).
- **Effective-date contingencies:** Sec. 6417 amendments take effect only on completion of rulemaking; Sec. 6201 requires rules within 270 days and sunsets the old pilot 270 days post-enactment.
- **Source note:** Sec. 6503 header is truncated in the source file ("Expansion of") but the operative text is "Expansion of 911 access."
