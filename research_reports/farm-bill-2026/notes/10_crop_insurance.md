# Title XI — Crop Insurance — Notes

## Title-level summary
Title XI amends the Federal Crop Insurance Act (FCIA, 7 U.S.C. 1501 et seq.) to expand and refine the Federal crop insurance program, with a heavy emphasis on specialty crops, new/expanded subsidized products, and program-integrity reforms to the relationship between USDA's Risk Management Agency (RMA)/Federal Crop Insurance Corporation (FCIC) and approved insurance providers (AIPs). Notably, this title is built primarily around governance, research-and-development mandates, studies, and process reforms rather than across-the-board premium subsidy rate increases — there is no general statutory subsidy-percentage hike in the engrossed text. The most concrete benefit increase is to beginning AND veteran farmers/ranchers (Sec. 11007), which extends existing beginning-farmer subsidy assistance to veteran farmers/ranchers and lengthens the veteran-farmer eligibility window from 5 to 10 years. A large research push (Sec. 11014) directs FCIC to develop and, in several cases, make available new revenue and index-based policies for oilseeds, alfalfa, pulses, sugarbeets, sugarcane, blueberries, wine grapes (smoke), mushrooms, frost/cold, hurricanes, and swine. Many provisions are STUDY-OR-REPORT mandates with statutory deadlines tied to enactment or crop years through 2031; reimbursement rates for AIP administrative/operating (A&O) costs are frozen at the 2026 level for 2027 and beyond (Sec. 11009).

## Section-by-section

### Sec. 11001 — Specialty Crop Advisory Committee
- **Does:** Creates a new Specialty Crop Advisory Committee; adds its Chairperson to the FCIC Board of Directors membership; adds Committee members to the Board's compensation/expense provisions; redesignates an existing Board member category from "specialty crop" to "livestock"; revises the Specialty Crops Coordinator's responsibilities; and requires the FCIC's annual review of new/specialty crops to be done in consultation with the new Committee.
- **Amends:** FCIA sec. 505 (7 U.S.C. 1505) — adds new subsection (f); sec. 507(g)(2) (7 U.S.C. 1507(g)(2)) — rewritten; sec. 508(a)(6)(A) (7 U.S.C. 1508(a)(6)(A)).
- **Numbers:** Committee = not fewer than 5 producers, not more than 10 total members; at least 1 producer from each of the West, Midwest, South, and Northeast regions (Census); meets not less than 2 times per year.
- **Dates:** Committee must be established not later than 180 days after enactment of the subsection.
- **Type:** NEW (governance body) / REFORM.
- **Affected:** Winners — specialty crop producers (new dedicated advisory voice on the Board and to the Manager). Who must act — Secretary (establish/appoint Committee), FCIC Board, Specialty Crops Coordinator.
- **Flag:** Heading change strikes "specialty crop" and inserts "livestock" for the redesignated Board member subparagraph (H) — shifts an existing Board seat description toward livestock while the specialty-crop role is now filled by the new Committee Chairperson.

### Sec. 11002 — Identification of holders of substantial interests
- **Does:** Lets the FCIC Manager require policyholders to report names of individuals/entities holding a substantial beneficial interest; grants an extension allowing late submission any time during the crop year, except where an AIP determines the policyholder would get disproportionate benefits from non-reporting or withheld info to evade a State/Federal-law obligation; raises the threshold defining a "substantial beneficial interest."
- **Amends:** FCIA sec. 506(m) (7 U.S.C. 1506(m)) — rewrites paragraph (3); amends paragraph (4).
- **Numbers:** Substantial beneficial interest threshold raised from **5 percent to 10 percent**.
- **Dates:** —
- **Type:** REFORM (eases reporting via extension; raises disclosure threshold).
- **Affected:** Winners — policyholders/larger ownership structures (higher 5%→10% threshold, late-filing grace). Who must act — FCIC Manager, AIPs (disproportionate-benefit/evasion determinations).
- **Flag:** Raising the disclosure threshold to 10% reduces transparency on ownership while the extension softens enforcement timing.

### Sec. 11003 — Actuarial soundness of certain new products
- **Does:** Requires FCIC to periodically review each policy/product developed under sec. 508(h) for actuarial soundness and to take actions (in consultation with submitters) to improve soundness.
- **Amends:** FCIA sec. 506(n) (7 U.S.C. 1506(n)) — adds new paragraph (4).
- **Numbers:** —
- **Dates:** "periodically" (no fixed interval).
- **Type:** REFORM (oversight requirement).
- **Affected:** FCIC must act; affects holders of 508(h) submitted products.
- **Flag:** —

### Sec. 11004 — Coverage of revenue losses
- **Does:** Expands the scope of insurable loss to include a decline in the market price of the insured commodity, so long as the decline was not directly caused by the producer (as determined by the Secretary).
- **Amends:** FCIA sec. 508(a)(1) (7 U.S.C. 1508(a)(1)) — adds language to the second sentence.
- **Numbers:** —
- **Dates:** —
- **Type:** REFORM/INCREASE (broadens insurable cause-of-loss to market-price declines).
- **Affected:** Winners — producers seeking revenue/price-decline protection. Who must act — Secretary (determinations).
- **Flag:** Statutory authorization for price-decline (revenue) coverage; pairs with Sec. 11014's revenue-policy expansions.

### Sec. 11005 — Limitation on farm program participation
- **Does:** Strikes "crops and" from a heading in sec. 508(c)(4)(C)(iv); sunsets the STAX (Stacked Income Protection Plan for upland cotton) effective-date provision to cover only the 2019–2025 crop years; and adds a conforming limitation barring a farm with STAX coverage from also receiving seed cotton PLC/ARC payments beginning with the 2026 crop year.
- **Amends:** FCIA sec. 508(c)(4)(C)(iv) and sec. 508B(f) (7 U.S.C. 1508b); conforming amendment to sec. 1115 of the Agricultural Act of 2014 (7 U.S.C. 9015) — adds new subsection (j).
- **Numbers:** —
- **Dates:** STAX effective window changed from "beginning with the 2019 crop year" to "2019 through 2025 crop years"; new STAX/seed-cotton payment limitation begins with the **2026 crop year**.
- **Type:** REFORM (anti-double-dipping limitation).
- **Affected:** Losers — upland cotton/seed cotton producers who would otherwise stack STAX with PLC (sec. 1116) or ARC (sec. 1117) on the same farm. Who must act — producers (must choose).
- **Flag:** Hard no-double-dip rule between STAX and seed cotton PLC/ARC.

### Sec. 11006 — Limitation on interest accrual
- **Does:** Caps the interest an AIP may charge a producer delinquent on a premium or administrative fee.
- **Amends:** FCIA sec. 508(d) (7 U.S.C. 1508(d)) — adds new paragraph (5).
- **Numbers:** Interest limited to ≤ **1 percent** of the simple interest of the delinquent amount per month, for not more than **60 consecutive months** of delinquency.
- **Dates:** Effective beginning with the **2026 reinsurance year**.
- **Type:** REFORM (caps/limits charges — benefits delinquent producers).
- **Affected:** Winners — delinquent producers (interest cap). Who must act — AIPs.
- **Flag:** —

### Sec. 11007 — Crop insurance support for beginning and veteran farmers and ranchers
- **Does:** Lengthens the period a producer can qualify as a "veteran farmer or rancher," and extends the enhanced premium-assistance benefit currently provided to beginning farmers/ranchers so it also covers veteran farmers/ranchers.
- **Amends:** FCIA sec. 502(b)(14)(B) (7 U.S.C. 1502(b)(14)(B)) — clauses (ii) and (iii); sec. 508(e)(9) (7 U.S.C. 1508(e)) — inserts "or veteran farmer or rancher" after "beginning farmer or rancher" each place it appears.
- **Numbers:** Veteran-farmer qualifying window extended from **5 years to 10 years** (and "5-year" → "10-year"). The dollar/percentage value of the beginning-farmer assistance itself is set by existing sec. 508(e)(9) (the additional premium subsidy / extra 10 percentage points historically afforded beginning farmers) and is here extended to veterans; the text does not restate a new percentage.
- **Dates:** —
- **Type:** INCREASE (expands subsidy assistance to a new class; lengthens eligibility).
- **Affected:** Winners — veteran farmers/ranchers (now get the beginning-farmer premium assistance) and veterans in years 6–10 of operation (now still "veteran" eligible). Who must act — RMA/FCIC.
- **Flag:** Key beginning/veteran-farmer subsidy boost in this title — extends enhanced premium assistance to veterans and doubles the veteran eligibility window.

### Sec. 11008 — Marketability
- **Does:** Revises the 508(h) submission process around marketability: clarifies confidentiality timing; allows an AIP that submits a letter of support to access product-development data (not treated as "the public") subject to confidentiality; adds a marketability deadline barring late-approved products (after the July 1 SRA closing date) from being implemented that reinsurance year unless training/handbook materials are released at least 90 days before the sales closing date; adds a marketability-determination process under which a product is "deemed marketable" if at least one AIP expresses support; and bars the Board from requiring an AIP letter of support to review/approve a product.
- **Amends:** FCIA sec. 508(h)(4) (7 U.S.C. 1508(h)(4)) — amends subparagraph (A)(iii), adds (D)(iv), adds new subparagraph (F).
- **Numbers:** Materials must be made available at least **90 days** before the sales closing date; SRA closing date referenced as **July 1**.
- **Dates:** Tied to reinsurance year / SRA July 1 closing date.
- **Type:** REFORM.
- **Affected:** Winners — AIPs/product submitters (easier marketability path; data access; one supporting AIP can deem a product marketable). Who must act — FCIC Board, AIPs.
- **Flag:** "Deemed marketable" if a single AIP supports it — a notably low bar that could accelerate new-product approvals.

### Sec. 11009 — Reimbursement rates for administrative and operating costs
- **Does:** Restructures the A&O cost-reimbursement rate caps; for the 2027 and subsequent reinsurance years sets the A&O reimbursement rate equal to the rate in effect for the 2026 reinsurance year.
- **Amends:** FCIA sec. 508(k)(4) (7 U.S.C. 1508(k)(4)) — amends subparagraph (A) (adds clause (iii)) and rewrites subparagraph (F).
- **Numbers:** No new percentage; rate for 2027+ = the 2026 reinsurance-year rate (freeze/peg).
- **Dates:** Applies to **2027 and subsequent reinsurance years** (2026 is the reference year; prior cap language "through 2026").
- **Type:** REFORM (freezes/pegs A&O reimbursement rate at 2026 level — effectively caps future growth).
- **Affected:** AIPs and agents (reimbursement pegged to 2026; no inflationary growth). Who must act — FCIC Board.
- **Flag:** Locks A&O reimbursement at 2026 levels indefinitely — a real-terms decline over time for AIPs/agents.

### Sec. 11010 — Quality loss adjustment coverage
- **Does:** Replaces the one-time quality-loss-adjustment (QLA) review with a recurring review cycle; requires regionally diverse stakeholder engagement; requires implementation of changes based on each review; and adds a congressional reporting requirement.
- **Amends:** FCIA sec. 508(m)(3) (7 U.S.C. 1508(m)(3)) — rewrites (A), amends (B), redesignates (B)→(C), inserts new (B), adds new (D).
- **Numbers:** Review **once every 5 years**; each review completed within **1 year** of initiation.
- **Dates:** Recurring reviews begin in **calendar year 2027** (and every 5 years thereafter).
- **Type:** STUDY-OR-REPORT / REFORM (recurring review + reporting).
- **Affected:** Winners — producers in regions with unique quality-loss issues. Who must act — FCIC (contract reviewer, report to House/Senate Ag Committees).
- **Flag:** Report must include plans for region-specific QLA procedures.

### Sec. 11011 — Pilot program to review effectiveness of coverage penalty
- **Does:** Creates a new freestanding FCIA section establishing a pilot program to evaluate the late-planting-period reduction-in-benefits penalty for corn and other crops; suspends the late-planting guarantee reduction within the pilot; analyzes whether days past final plant date correlate with yield decline; and studies whether late planting reduces Ogallala Aquifer irrigation use.
- **Amends:** FCIA (7 U.S.C. 1501 et seq.) — inserts new sec. 508E after sec. 508D ("New freestanding provision" within FCIA).
- **Numbers:** Conducted in not fewer than **10 counties** in/adjacent to the North Plains or Panhandle Groundwater Conservation Districts (Texas); operate for not fewer than **4 crop years**; up to **$200,000** (from sec. 522(e)(2)(A)(ii) funds) for a partnership/cooperative agreement.
- **Dates:** Effective beginning with the **2027 crop year**; report not later than **90 days after the last day of crop year 2031**.
- **Type:** NEW (pilot program) / STUDY-OR-REPORT.
- **Affected:** Winners — Texas High Plains producers planting in the late period (suspended penalty during pilot). Who must act — RMA and FCIC; report to House Ag Committee and Senate Committee on Agriculture, Forestry, and Nutrition (note: bill's wording orders the words differently).
- **Flag:** Geographically targeted (Texas Panhandle/Ogallala Aquifer); ties crop insurance penalty to groundwater conservation.

### Sec. 11012 — Whole farm improvements
- **Does:** Requires FCIC to annually review the insurable-revenue limitations under Whole-Farm Revenue Protection (including overall and animal/animal-product/greenhouse-nursery/aquaculture-specific limits) to ensure adequacy for high-value products, and to report on each review and expected policy changes.
- **Amends:** FCIA sec. 522(c)(7)(E) (7 U.S.C. 1522(c)(7)(E)) — adds new clause (iii).
- **Numbers:** —
- **Dates:** First review not later than **12 months** after enactment of the clause, and **annually** thereafter.
- **Type:** STUDY-OR-REPORT / REFORM.
- **Affected:** Winners — high-value/diversified, livestock, nursery, and aquaculture producers using Whole-Farm Revenue Protection. Who must act — FCIC (report to House/Senate Ag Committees).
- **Flag:** Annual cadence could lead to raised Whole-Farm revenue limits over time.

### Sec. 11013 — Program compliance and integrity
- **Does:** Builds a structured multi-stage notification/appeal/determination process for compliance findings against AIPs (initial finding → AIP appeal → final finding → request for final administrative determination → final determination → appeal to Civilian Board of Contract Appeals); ties debt relief to FCIC's compliance with timing rules (with an OIG/DOJ exception); and makes a "final agency determination" on certain inquiries "binding."
- **Amends:** FCIA sec. 515(b) (7 U.S.C. 1515(b)) — heading and paragraphs (1)–(3); sec. 506(r)(1) (7 U.S.C. 1506(r)(1)) — inserts "binding."
- **Numbers:** Each stage governed by **90-day** windows (90-day AIP appeal; FCIC final finding within 90 days; 90 days to request final administrative determination; FCIC final determination within 90 days unless substantial new info; 90 days to appeal to the Civilian Board of Contract Appeals).
- **Dates:** —
- **Type:** REFORM (due-process/appeals structure for AIPs).
- **Affected:** Winners — AIPs (clear appeal rights; debt relief if FCIC misses deadlines). Who must act — FCIC (must meet 90-day deadlines or forgive debt, except OIG/DOJ matters).
- **Flag:** Failure of FCIC to timely notify relieves AIP of debt owed — a strong incentive on the agency, weakening recovery of overpayments.

### Sec. 11014 — Research and development priorities
- **Does:** Adds eight new R&D mandates (paragraphs (20)–(28)) directing FCIC to research/develop — and in several cases make available — new policies. Subsections: (a) expansion of revenue policies; (b) wine grape smoke-exposure losses; (c) mushrooms; (d) hurricane/tropical-storm standalone study; (e) frost/cold-weather index policy; (f) double/rotational cropping of oilseeds study; (g) harvest incentives; (h) prevented planting for non-perennial specialty crops; (i) swine catastrophic-event policy.
- **Amends:** FCIA sec. 522(c) (7 U.S.C. 1522(c)) — adds paragraphs (20), (21), (22), (23), (24), (25), (26), (27), (28).
- **Numbers/Crops:** (20) revenue policies for oilseeds (camelina, carinata, pennycress), alfalfa, pulse crops (incl. dry edible beans), sugarbeets, sugarcane, blueberries, plus other yield-only crops; (21) wine grapes incl. CA/OR/WA; (24) frost/cold index — table/wine/juice grapes, tomatoes, peppers, sugarcane, strawberries, melons, citrus, peaches, blueberries, etc.; (25) covered oilseed crops = rapeseed, canola, camelina, others. Several "make available" mandates notwithstanding the last sentence of sec. 508(a)(1) and sec. 508(a)(2).
- **Dates (deadlines after enactment of each paragraph):** (20) report ≤18 months; (21) R&D ≤1 year, make available ≤18 months, report ≤2 years; (22) report ≤2 years; (23) report ≤1 year; (24) report ≤1 year; (25) report ≤13 months; (26) R&D ≤1 year, make available ≤24 months, report ≤1 year; (27) R&D ≤1 year, report ≤18 months; (28) report ≤1 year.
- **Type:** NEW / INCREASE (expansion of subsidized products) / STUDY-OR-REPORT.
- **Affected:** Winners — producers of the named specialty/oilseed/livestock commodities; wine grape growers in fire-prone West; mushroom growers; swine producers. Who must act — FCIC (R&D, mandatory make-available where specified, reports to House/Senate Ag Committees; (21)/(26) also Appropriations Committees).
- **Flag:** Largest expansion of subsidized product availability in the title; several paragraphs MANDATE that FCIC make the new policy available (not merely study it), overriding the usual sec. 508(a)(1)/(a)(2) constraints — revenue coverage for oilseeds/alfalfa/pulses/sugar/blueberries, wine-grape smoke, mushrooms, and harvest incentives.

### Sec. 11015 — Report on Standard Reinsurance Agreement
- **Does:** Requires FCIC to report on the Standard Reinsurance Agreement (SRA), analyzing modifications to expand availability of policies/plans, including additional reinsurance funds for high-loss-ratio States and for area-plan AIPs, A&O fund obligation methods, and agent compensation consistent with historical norms.
- **Amends:** New freestanding provision (references FCIA sec. 508(k)(8)(F) budget-neutrality requirement and sec. 507(c) A&O reimbursements).
- **Numbers:** —
- **Dates:** Report not later than **90 days** after enactment.
- **Type:** STUDY-OR-REPORT.
- **Affected:** AIPs, agents, producers in high-loss-ratio States and area-plan markets. Who must act — FCIC (consult producers, agents, AIPs, House/Senate Ag Committees; report).
- **Flag:** Explores additional reinsurance funds for area-plan (e.g., SCO/area-based) AIPs and high-loss-ratio States — could reshape risk-sharing economics.

### Sec. 11016 — Hurricane insurance protection-wind index report
- **Does:** Requires FCIC to report on the Hurricane Insurance Protection–Wind Index (HIP-WI), analyzing NOAA weather-radio-station outages in the preceding 5 years that caused producers to lose coverage, and a contingency plan to obtain data from land-grant institutions or other third parties.
- **Amends:** New freestanding provision (references National Agricultural Research, Extension, and Teaching Policy Act of 1977 sec. 1404, 7 U.S.C. 3103).
- **Numbers:** 5-year lookback period.
- **Dates:** Report not later than **1 year** after enactment.
- **Type:** STUDY-OR-REPORT.
- **Affected:** Winners — hurricane-exposed producers relying on HIP-WI. Who must act — FCIC (consult NOAA Administrator; report to House/Senate Ag Committees).
- **Flag:** Addresses single-point-of-failure dependence on NOAA weather-radio data for triggering index payouts.

### Sec. 11017 — Risk management study for lamb
- **Does:** Directs the Secretary to study modifications to existing livestock protection/risk-management programs to enhance protection for domestic lamb producers, accounting for market access, feed sourcing/costs, imports, demand/trends, labor costs, and market-data availability/accuracy.
- **Amends:** New freestanding provision (references existing programs incl. dairy margin coverage, livestock risk protection).
- **Numbers:** —
- **Dates:** Report not later than **1 year** after enactment.
- **Type:** STUDY-OR-REPORT.
- **Affected:** Winners — domestic lamb producers. Who must act — Secretary (report to House/Senate Ag Committees).
- **Flag:** —

### Sec. 11018 — Study on livestock risk protection policy (feeder cattle / adverse weather)
- **Does:** Directs the Secretary to study modifications to the Livestock Risk Protection (LRP) policy under FCIA sec. 523(b) to improve flexibility for feeder-cattle producers affected by adverse weather (drought, wildfire), including whether current penalty/endorsement structures deter early marketing and whether to provide flexibility/exemptions.
- **Amends:** New freestanding provision (references FCIA sec. 523(b), 7 U.S.C. 1523(b)).
- **Numbers:** Focus on producers marketing feeder cattle more than **60 days** prior to a coverage-endorsement end date.
- **Dates:** Report not later than **1 year** after enactment.
- **Type:** STUDY-OR-REPORT.
- **Affected:** Winners — feeder-cattle producers facing drought/wildfire forced-marketing decisions. Who must act — Secretary (report to House/Senate Ag Committees).
- **Flag:** —

## Cross-cutting flags for this title
- No general/across-the-board statutory premium-subsidy percentage increase appears in the engrossed text. The one concrete subsidy-assistance expansion is extending the existing beginning-farmer premium assistance (sec. 508(e)(9)) to veteran farmers/ranchers and lengthening veteran eligibility from 5 to 10 years (Sec. 11007). There is no SCO/area-plan subsidy-rate hike or coverage-level raise stated in the text; Sec. 11015 only studies area-plan reinsurance funds.
- Specialty crops are the dominant theme: new advisory committee (11001), revised Specialty Crops Coordinator (11001), prevented-planting R&D for specialty crops (11014(h)), and numerous specialty-crop product R&D mandates (11014).
- Expansion of subsidized products is concentrated in Sec. 11014, where several paragraphs MANDATE that FCIC make new policies available (oilseeds/alfalfa/pulses/sugarbeets/sugarcane/blueberries revenue; wine-grape smoke; mushrooms; harvest incentives), overriding sec. 508(a)(1)/(a)(2) limits.
- Revenue/price-decline coverage is broadened generally (Sec. 11004) and specifically (Sec. 11014(20)).
- AIP-favorable process reforms: marketability "deemed marketable" on a single AIP's support (11008); structured 90-day appeal chain with debt relief if FCIC misses deadlines (11013); interest-accrual cap protecting delinquent producers (11006).
- A&O reimbursement frozen at 2026 levels for 2027+ (11009) — a cost-control/AIP-revenue-constraining measure that cuts against the otherwise AIP-friendly tone.
- Reauthorization horizon: program elements run through FY2031 (e.g., pilot report tied to crop year 2031 in Sec. 11011); recurring QLA reviews begin CY2027 (11010).
- Heavy reliance on STUDY-OR-REPORT mandates (11010–11018 and most of 11014), nearly all reporting to the House Committee on Agriculture and the Senate Committee on Agriculture, Nutrition, and Forestry (Sec. 11011 uniquely names the Senate committee as "Agriculture, Forestry, and Nutrition"; Secs. 11014(b),(g) also loop in Appropriations Committees).
- Disclosure threshold for substantial beneficial interest raised 5%→10% (11002) reduces ownership transparency.
- Targeted/geographic provisions: Texas High Plains late-planting/Ogallala pilot (11011); wine-grape smoke focus on CA/OR/WA (11014(b)).
