# Title II — Conservation — Notes

## Title-level summary
Title II reauthorizes the major Food Security Act of 1985 (FSA) conservation programs through FY2031, with the dominant through-line being a standard 5-year extension (mostly "striking 2023 and inserting 2031" / "2019 through 2023" → "2027 through 2031") plus three substantive policy themes: (1) embedding "precision agriculture" as a defined, prioritized, and cost-share-enhanced category across EQIP and CSP (payments up to 90% of cost); (2) a sweeping new emphasis on "wildlife habitat connectivity" / wildlife migration corridors woven through EQIP, CSP, RCPP, and general administration; and (3) major structural easement reform — creating a brand-new standalone **Forest Conservation Easement Program (FCEP)** as new subtitle I of title XII (absorbing and repealing the Healthy Forests Reserve Program), which bumps the legacy Regional Conservation Partnership Program (RCPP) to subtitle J. Notable new money: CSP/CRP/EQIP CCC funding is restated with EQIP rising to **$2.53B (FY27) → $3.255B (FY31)**, RCPP set at **$450M/yr**, the new FCEP funded **$25M → $65M/yr**, a new **$100M/yr State/Tribal soil-health grant program**, feral swine control extended at **$150M (FY25–31)**, and a new EQIP **Southern Border Initiative**. ACEP is reformed with new wetland easement stewardship payments (up to 100% of costs), a 25% "lower cost-share" easement option, and an AGI exemption for easement compensation. The source text (an FSA reauthorization vehicle) contains **no rescission, redirection, or rollback of Inflation Reduction Act (IRA) conservation funds** — IRA is never mentioned in this title; all funding cited is Commodity Credit Corporation (CCC) farm-bill baseline money.

## Section-by-section

### Sec. 2001 — Definitions
- **Does:** Adds new statutory definitions to the FSA conservation title: "precision agriculture," "precision agriculture technology" (with a 12-item list of qualifying technologies A–L: GPS/geospatial mapping, satellite/aerial imagery, yield monitors, soil mapping, sensors, IoT/telematics, data/analytics software, connectivity, GPS guidance/auto-steer, variable-rate/section control, immersive technologies, and a catch-all), and "wildlife habitat connectivity." Conforms cross-references (subtitles A–I → A–J to accommodate new FCEP subtitle), updates "Indian tribe/Indian Tribe" terminology, and renumbers paragraphs.
- **Amends:** FSA §1201(a) (16 U.S.C. 3801(a)).
- **Numbers:** —
- **Dates:** —
- **Type:** NEW (definitions) / REFORM
- **Affected:** Sets up precision-ag and wildlife-connectivity priorities used throughout the title; producers and equipment vendors benefit.
- **Flag:** "Precision agriculture technology" definition is broad and vendor-friendly; new through-line for the whole title.

### Sec. 2202 — Establishment and administration (EQIP)
- **Does:** Multiple EQIP reforms. (a) Adds wildlife habitat connectivity to foregone-income payment eligibility; clarifies producers receiving EQIP payments may also receive a Consolidated Farm and Rural Development Act §304 loan/guarantee for the same practice/land, with written notice; renames and expands "high-priority practices" to "state-determined high-priority" and adds two new qualifying categories: wildlife habitat/connectivity/migration corridors, and increased carbon sequestration / reduced GHG (methane, nitrous oxide). Adds **increased payments for precision agriculture practices up to 90% of costs** (new para (8)). Adds cost-share payments for grassland enrolled in CRP for wildlife-corridor purposes (new para (9)), with anti-double-dipping limitation. (b) Extends EQIP funding allocation authority through FY2031. (c) Adds precision-ag adoption/technology to water-conservation/irrigation-efficiency practices. (d) Raises the organic-production EQIP payment cap. (e) Adds precision ag to conservation incentive contracts. (f) Creates a new **Southern Border Initiative** (new subsec (k)): payments to producers to repair "covered damage" (damage to ag land or farming infrastructure) on "covered land" (counties at/near the southern border); 1-year contract term.
- **Amends:** FSA §1240B(d)(3)(F), (d)(6), (d)(7), (d)(8) new, (d)(9) new, (f)(1), (h)(1), (i)(3)(A)(ii), (j)(2)(A)(i), (k) new (16 U.S.C. 3839aa-2).
- **Numbers:** Precision-ag payments up to **90% of costs**. Organic-production payment cap raised from **$140,000 (FY2019–2023) to $200,000 (FY2027–2031)**. Southern Border Initiative contract term **1 year**.
- **Dates:** FY2027–2031 (organic cap); through FY2031 (funding allocation).
- **Type:** REFORM / INCREASE / NEW (Southern Border Initiative, precision-ag payments, grassland wildlife-corridor cost-share)
- **Affected:** EQIP producers; precision-ag and organic producers win; southern-border-county producers gain new payment stream; carbon/GHG and wildlife-corridor practices elevated.
- **Flag:** New Southern Border Initiative is notable/novel; 90% precision-ag cost-share is unusually high vs. normal EQIP cost-share.

### Sec. 2602 — Agricultural land easements (ACEP-ALE)
- **Does:** Reforms agricultural land easement cost-share. Sets Federal share **≤65%** of fair market value (FMV) of the easement (determined via USPAP, areawide market analysis, or industry-approved method); **≤90%** for socially disadvantaged farmers/ranchers (≥50% ownership); **≤75%** for grassland of special environmental significance. Requires non-Federal share to equal the remainder. Adds a new **"Lower cost-share option"**: an eligible entity may take a Federal share **≤25%** without a Secretary right-of-enforcement (using its own terms/conditions), but must provide cash resources **≥50%** of FMV and retain its own right of enforcement. Allows separate (pooled) ranking of socially-disadvantaged applicants. Rewrites agreement terms (Secretary right of enforcement narrowed; inspection only on missed monitoring reports or articulable belief of violation, with notice; transfer-to-different-entity right if entity dissolves/becomes ineligible). Reforms certification of eligible entities (reduces certain thresholds **10 → 5**; new certification pathway for entities that acquired **≥10** easements and met responsibilities; adds annual quality easement-review process with waiver for demonstrated compliance).
- **Amends:** FSA §1265B(a), (b)(2), (b)(3), (b)(4), (b)(5) (16 U.S.C. 3865b).
- **Numbers:** Federal share **≤65%** (general), **≤90%** (socially disadvantaged ≥50% owner), **≤75%** (special grassland); lower cost-share option Federal share **≤25%** with **≥50%** FMV cash from entity; certification thresholds **10→5**; new pathway requires **≥10** prior easements.
- **Dates:** —
- **Type:** REFORM / NEW (lower cost-share option, easement quality review)
- **Affected:** ACEP eligible entities (land trusts); socially disadvantaged landowners; grassland owners.
- **Flag:** New 25% lower-cost-share / no-Secretary-enforcement option is notable structural change.

### Sec. 2603 — Wetland reserve easements (ACEP-WRE)
- **Does:** Adds socially-disadvantaged farmers/ranchers to a wetland-easement priority and allows pooled ranking of their offers. Creates a new **easement stewardship** financial-assistance regime: USDA shall fund repair, necessary maintenance, and enhancement per wetland reserve easement plans; regularly assess maintenance/management needs; create/update a stewardship strategy; establish a 5-year schedule; pay **up to 100% of eligible costs**; report to Ag Committees within 2 years. Broadens "technical assistance" to "assistance" and allows contracts/agreements with federal/state/local agencies, NGOs, Tribes, private entities for restoration/maintenance. Reserves **≥15%** of section funds for the wetland reserve enhancement option.
- **Amends:** FSA §1265C(b), (c), (d) new, (e), (f) (16 U.S.C. 3865c).
- **Numbers:** Stewardship payments **up to 100%** of eligible costs; **5-year** schedule; report in **2 years**; **≥15%** of funds reserved for wetland reserve enhancement option.
- **Dates:** Report within 2 years of enactment.
- **Type:** NEW (stewardship payments) / REFORM
- **Affected:** Wetland reserve easement landowners; socially disadvantaged farmers; restoration contractors/NGOs.
- **Flag:** New 100%-cost stewardship payments and 15% set-aside are notable.

### Sec. 2701 — Forest conservation easement program (NEW PROGRAM — subtitle I)
- **Does:** Creates a brand-new **Forest Conservation Easement Program (FCEP)** as new subtitle I of title XII FSA (§§1267–1267D), redesignating the old subtitle I (RCPP) as subtitle J. FCEP conserves/restores forest land via conservation easements, with two tracks: **(a) Forest land easements** — cost-share to eligible entities to buy easements on working forest land (Federal share **50%**, up to **75%** for forests of special environmental significance or socially disadvantaged forest landowners); permanent or max-State-duration easements; agreements 3–5 years; allows landowner-retained working forest use, forest management plans, and (conditionally) subsurface mineral development (no surface mining). **(b) Forest reserve easements** — conveyed to the Secretary for species at risk; permanent, 30-year, or max-State-duration easements (and 30-year contracts for Tribal land); **≤10%** of annual funds for 30-year easements; compensation = FMV difference (permanent) or 50–75% thereof (other); financial assistance up to 100% (permanent) or 50–75% (other) of eligible restoration costs, **capped at $500,000 per easement/contract**; ESA safe-harbor assurances. Administration mirrors ACEP (subordination/modification/exchange/termination, ineligible land, 90-day congressional notice before termination). Absorbs Healthy Forests Reserve Program land.
- **Amends:** New subtitle I (§§1267–1267D) added to title XII FSA (16 U.S.C. 3801 et seq.); redesignates old subtitle I (16 U.S.C. 3871 et seq.) as subtitle J.
- **Numbers:** Forest land easement Federal share **50%** (up to **75%** exception); forest reserve compensation **50–75%** of permanent value for non-permanent; financial assistance up to **100%** (permanent) / **50–75%** (other); **≤10%** of funds for 30-year easements; **$500,000** per-easement financial-assistance cap. (Program-level funding provided via §1241(a)(5): $25M→$65M/yr FY27–31, per Sec. 2501.)
- **Dates:** —
- **Type:** NEW (program creation) / REFORM (structural reorganization)
- **Affected:** Private forest landowners; Indian Tribes; land trusts/eligible entities; at-risk species.
- **Flag:** Major new standalone forest easement program absorbing HFRP; bumps RCPP to subtitle J.

### Sec. 2702 — Healthy Forests Reserve Program (REPEAL)
- **Does:** Repeals title V of the Healthy Forests Restoration Act of 2003 (the HFRP) and conforms its table of contents. Preserves validity of existing HFRP contracts/agreements/easements and required payments/TA. Allows prior-year (FY2019–2025) CCC funds and new FCEP funds to be used to continue existing HFRP contracts (no payment increase via modification).
- **Amends:** Repeals title V of Healthy Forests Restoration Act of 2003 (16 U.S.C. 6571 et seq.); conforms its table of contents.
- **Numbers:** Prior-year funds FY2019–2025 preserved for legacy contracts.
- **Dates:** FY2019–2025 (prior-year funds).
- **Type:** REPEAL / EXTEND (transitional)
- **Affected:** Existing HFRP participants (rolled into FCEP); future forest-easement applicants.
- **Flag:** HFRP repealed and folded into the new FCEP.

### Sec. 2805 — Funding (RCPP)
- **Does:** Restructures RCPP funding allocation: strikes old subsections (a) and (b), redesignates (c)/(d)/(e) as (a)/(b)/(c), ties funding to §1241(a)(6) (the new $450M/yr line). ## Cross-cutting flags for this title

- **CRP acreage caps (Sec. 2101):** Existing CRP statutory acreage cap is *extended* through FY2031 (not raised). Continuous-enrollment floor preserved at **not fewer than 8,600,000 acres** by Sept. 30, 2031. SAFE-initiative land newly eligible for continuous enrollment. County enrollment cap stays at **25% of cropland** (CRP + wetland reserve easements) per Sec. 2503.
- **EQIP (Sec. 2201–2205, 2501):** Funded at **$2.53B (FY27) rising to $3.255B (FY31)** per Sec. 2501. New precision-ag cost-share **up to 90%**; new Southern Border Initiative; new grassland wildlife-corridor cost-share; carbon/GHG and wildlife-corridor added as state-determined high-priority practices; organic payment cap **$140K → $200K**.
- **CSP (Sec. 2301–2302, 2501):** New **$4,000 minimum annual payment**; precision-ag added to supplemental payments; new CRP-grassland wildlife-corridor cost-share with emergency grazing/haying protection.
- **ACEP (Sec. 2601–2604, 2501):** New **25% lower cost-share / no-Secretary-enforcement** ALE option; Federal share ≤65% general (≤90% socially disadvantaged, ≤75% special grassland); new **wetland easement stewardship payments up to 100%** of costs with **≥15%** funds reserved for wetland reserve enhancement; new **AGI exemption** for easement compensation; de minimis adjustment authority; NEPA carve-out for modifications.
- **RCPP (Sec. 2801–2807, 2501):** Bumped from subtitle I to **subtitle J**; funded at **$450M/yr** FY27–31; streamlined agreements (180 days) and payments (30 days); ≥50% direct-funding requirement; new regulatory-adjustment/waiver authority; wildlife-connectivity added to critical conservation areas; Columbia Basin project area added to Western Waters Region CCA.
- **NEW programs:** (1) **State assistance for soil health** — **$100M/yr** FY27–31 grants to States/Tribes (Sec. 2303); (2) **Forest Conservation Easement Program (FCEP)** — new subtitle I, **$25M→$65M/yr** FY27–31, absorbing HFRP (Sec. 2701/2501); (3) **EQIP Southern Border Initiative** (Sec. 2202); (4) **Feral swine eradication program** made permanent, **$150M FY25–31** (Sec. 2402); (5) **Farmer-to-farmer network assistance** and **non-Federal certifying entity** TA systems plus **NRCS direct-hire authority** (Sec. 2502).
- **REPEALS:** Healthy Forests Reserve Program (title V, Healthy Forests Restoration Act of 2003) repealed and folded into FCEP (Sec. 2702); 2018 Act feral-swine pilot (§2408) repealed (Sec. 2402).
- **IRA conservation funds:** **No mention of the Inflation Reduction Act anywhere in Title II.** There is NO rescission, redirection, or rollover of IRA conservation money into the farm-bill baseline within this title's text. All funding cited is Commodity Credit Corporation (CCC) farm-bill mandatory money or program appropriations. (If IRA rescission/rollover occurs in this bill, it is not located in Title II as provided.)
- **Through-line themes:** "Precision agriculture" (defined, prioritized, up to 90% cost-share across EQIP/CSP); "wildlife habitat connectivity / migration corridors" (woven across EQIP, CSP, RCPP, general admin); composting newly designated a conservation practice/activity; streamlining of technical assistance, practice standards, and easement administration.
