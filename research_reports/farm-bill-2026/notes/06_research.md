# Title VII — Research, Extension, and Related Matters — Notes

## Title-level summary
Title VII reauthorizes USDA's research, extension, education, and economics (REE) programs — the bulk of which sit in the National Agricultural Research, Extension, and Teaching Policy Act of 1977 (NARETPA), the Food, Agriculture, Conservation, and Trade Act of 1990 (FACTA), the Agricultural Research, Extension, and Education Reform Act of 1998 (AREERA), and the 2008 farm bill — by extending almost every expiring authorization from FY2023 to FY2031. Beyond the routine reauthorizations, the title delivers several substantive boosts to 1890 land-grant institutions (raising the Evans-Allen/extension funding floors from 20–30% to 40% and tightening state matching enforcement via gubernatorial attestations), expands 1994 Tribal-college equipment authority, and reforms AGARDA, the Centers of Excellence, the Specialty Crop Research Initiative (with a new $30M/yr mechanization-and-automation carve-out), and the AFRI (Agriculture and Food Research Initiative). It creates new freestanding programs and studies, including a Researching the Transition to Organic grant ($7.5M/yr), veteran-farmer education grants ($3M/yr), an Agricultural Innovation Corps, a NASS Modernization Commission, and broad interagency-coordination authority for the Under Secretary for REE (MOUs with DOE, NSF, DOD, HHS). Notable national-security/animal-welfare provisions cap REE research collaboration with China/Russia and other countries of concern and limit painful research on dogs and cats. The title leans heavily on precision agriculture, biosecurity/cybersecurity, aquaculture, and specialty-crop themes throughout.

### Sec. 7125 — Agriculture Advanced Research and Development Authority (AGARDA)
- **Does:** Major reform. Adds precision agriculture to equipment scope and defines "precision agriculture" (managing/tracking/reducing crop or livestock production inputs — seed, feed, fertilizer, chemicals, water, time — at heightened spatial/temporal granularity to improve efficiency, reduce waste, maintain environmental quality). Replaces "person"/"qualified person" terminology with "eligible entity" throughout. Broadens covered products/projects ("any other product or project as determined by the Secretary"). Adds new mission purposes: overcoming long-term/high-risk technological barriers for technologies enhancing export competitiveness, environmental sustainability, water conservation, resilience to extreme weather/drought/disease/pests; and enhancing the role of sustainable agriculture in voluntary resilience solutions (extreme-weather crop impact, drought/soil water-holding capacity, long-term carbon storage, sustainable/biofuel energy, voluntary carbon-sequestering conservation, precision-ag adoption). Requires the Secretary to use the strategic plan to inform AGARDA administration. **Extends AGARDA's lifespan from 5 years to 13 years** (and the strategic-plan horizon 5-year→13-year). Reauthorizes.
- **Amends:** NARETPA §1473H (7 U.S.C. 3319k).
- **Numbers:** AGARDA duration 5 yr→13 yr; plan 5-yr→13-yr.
- **Dates:** 2023→2031.
- **Type:** REFORM / EXTEND (significant).
- **Affected:** Ag-tech researchers/startups, precision-ag developers (winners).
- **Flag:** NOTABLE — large lifespan extension (5→13 yrs) plus precision-ag definition added.

## Cross-cutting flags for this title
- **Universal reauthorization to FY2031:** the dominant mechanic is "strike 2023, insert 2031." The vast majority of sections are pure extensions; the substance is concentrated in a minority of reform/new sections.
- **1890 land-grant institutions are major winners:** §7110 raises the extension floor 20%→40% and the Evans-Allen research floor 30%→40%; §7114 rewrites the 1:1 state match requirement; §7508 adds gubernatorial attestation + public reporting to enforce the match. These four sections interlock.
- **1994 Tribal institutions gain equipment/capacity authority:** §7501 ("equal to"→"not less than" endowment floor + equipment use) and §7510 (Smith-Lever equipment authority); §7115 carves 1994s out of certain definitions.
- **Precision agriculture is a recurring theme:** defined and embedded in AGARDA (§7125), Centers of Excellence (§7208), AFRI (§7503), and the DOD MOU focus areas (§7608); also specialty-crop mechanization/automation appears in §7204(25), §7305 ($30M/yr), and §7208.
- **Foreign-influence / research-security:** §7614 prohibits REE vertebrate-animal research with China/Russia/countries-of-concern (CHIPS Act definition); §7608(h)(4) requires CHIPS-Act research-security compliance for all new interagency activities; §7601 imposes new gift/grant/contract disclosure on FFAR.
