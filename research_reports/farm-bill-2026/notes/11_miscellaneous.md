# Title XII — Miscellaneous Provisions — Notes

## Title-level summary
Title XII is the catch-all title of H.R. 7567 EH, organized into five subtitles: (A) Livestock and Other Animals — split into Part I (Animal Health and Production) and Part II (Meat and Poultry Processing and Inspection); (B) Department of Agriculture Reorganization Act of 1994; (C) National Security; (D) Other Miscellaneous Provisions; and (E) United States Grain Standards Reauthorization. It reauthorizes core animal-health programs (NAHLN, NADPRP, the vaccine/countermeasures bank) at FY2027–2031, and contains several high-profile substantive policy moves: a federal preemption of state "patchwork" production standards for livestock-derived products (the Proposition 12 response), a federal ban on commercial greyhound racing, a near-total rebuild of foreign agricultural land disclosure/enforcement (AFIDA overhaul, CFIUS membership for USDA, a prohibition on purchases by foreign adversaries), and expansion of USDA farm loans to commercial fishing and fish processing. The title also creates new offices (Office of Seafood), numerous studies/reports, and reauthorizes the U.S. Grain Standards Act through FY2033. Many provisions are freestanding (studies, working groups), while the substantive ones amend the Animal Health Protection Act, Animal Welfare Act, AFIDA, the Defense Production Act (CFIUS), the Consolidated Farm and Rural Development Act, and the Grain Standards Act. Notable controversies: livestock interstate-commerce preemption (Prop 12), greyhound racing ban, farm-equipment Clean Air Act exemption, and qualified renewable biomass carbon-neutrality treatment.

## Section-by-section

### Sec. 12001 — Animal disease prevention and management
- **Does:** Amends NADPRP activities to add "improving animal disease traceability" as an eligible activity; clarifies that "other activities approved by the Secretary as of enactment" qualify. Reauthorizes NAHLN, NADPRP, and the National Animal Vaccine and Veterinary Countermeasures Bank. Expands the administrative-cost set-aside to cover both NAHLN (subsection (a)) and NADPRP (subsection (b)) and raises the admin cap from 10% to 15%.
- **Amends:** Animal Health Protection Act sec. 10409A(b)(2)(F),(I), (d)(2)(A), (d)(2)(B), (d)(3)(B), (e)(1) (7 U.S.C. 8308a)
- **Numbers:** Admin cost cap raised 10% → 15%
- **Dates:** Reauthorizations changed "2019 through 2023" → "2027 through 2031" (multiple subsections)
- **Type:** EXTEND/REFORM/INCREASE
- **Affected:** APHIS; state/animal-health labs; livestock producers (FMD vaccine bank funding continues)
- **Flag:** FMD/vaccine bank and NAHLN funding continuity; admin cap increase is a 50% relative bump

### Sec. 12002 — Cattle Fever Tick Eradication Program review and report

### Sec. 12005 — Importation of live dogs
- **Does:** Adds new sec. 10404A to the Animal Health Protection Act governing dog imports. Requires electronic documentation before transport showing dog is healthy, vaccinated/parasite-treated with negative tests (vet certificate), permanently identified, and (if for transfer) at least 6 months old and accompanied by an import permit. Lists exceptions (returning US-origin pets, military working dogs, research, veterinary treatment then re-export, and under-6-month dogs imported to Hawaii from British Isles/Australia/Guam/New Zealand). Requires regulations within 18 months; centralized database; data-sharing with state veterinarians within 3 days; annual public reporting on countries of origin/purposes; fee authority. Enforcement via sec. 10414 penalties; importers bear cost of care/forfeiture/quarantine/removal/return. Transfers regulatory authority from the Animal Welfare Act to APHIS.
- **Amends:** Animal Health Protection Act (inserts sec. 10404A after 7 U.S.C. 8303); REPEALS sec. 18 of Animal Welfare Act (7 U.S.C. 2148); transitional savings clause for old AWA regs
- **Numbers:** Transfer-dog minimum age = 6 months; state-vet data share within 3 days
- **Dates:** Regulations within 18 months of enactment
- **Type:** NEW/REFORM/REPEAL
- **Affected:** Dog importers/transporters; rescue/adoption importers; HHS/Commerce/DHS/DOT (consulted); state veterinarians
- **Flag:** Moves live-dog import authority from AWA to AHPA; fee-funded; broad documentation/database regime

### Sec. 12006 — Ensuring the free movement of livestock-derived products in interstate commerce
- **Does:** Declares a federal right for covered-livestock producers to raise and market in interstate commerce, and bars any State (or subdivision) from enacting or enforcing — directly or indirectly — any production condition/standard on covered livestock not physically raised in that state, or any production condition/standard (as a condition of sale or consumption) on products derived from covered livestock not raised in that state that is "in addition to, or different from" the producing state's standards. "Covered livestock" = domestic animals raised for slaughter for human consumption or for milk/dairy products; EXCLUDES animals raised primarily for egg production. "Production" = raising/breeding, not movement/harvest/processing.
- **Amends:** New freestanding provision (federal preemption)
- **Numbers:** —
- **Dates:** —
- **Type:** NEW/REFORM (preemption)
- **Affected:** WINNERS: out-of-state livestock/pork/dairy producers, packers. LOSERS: states with animal-welfare production mandates (e.g., California Prop 12, Massachusetts Question 3) and the standards they impose on imported products.
- **Flag:** CONTROVERSIAL — this is the bill's Proposition 12 / EATS-Act-style preemption. Notably excludes eggs from "covered livestock" (so Prop 12 egg provisions are not directly reached). Targets dairy and slaughter livestock (pork especially).

- **Flag:** —

### Sec. 12008 — Protection of greyhounds
- **Does:** Adds new sec. 30 to the Animal Welfare Act making it unlawful to: engage in commercial greyhound racing involving interstate/foreign commerce; conduct racing/meetings with betting; engage in/facilitate simulcast betting in interstate/foreign commerce; or sell/buy/possess/train/transport/deliver/receive a greyhound for commercial racing. Secretary investigates (may use FBI, Treasury, other LE). Rule of construction preserves state gambling/animal-welfare laws and the Interstate Horseracing Act.
- **Amends:** Animal Welfare Act (7 U.S.C. 2131 et seq.) — new sec. 30
- **Numbers:** Penalty: fine + imprisonment up to 7 years per violation
- **Dates:** Applies to conduct on/after October 1, 2027
- **Type:** NEW (federal prohibition)
- **Affected:** Greyhound racing industry (effectively a national ban); simulcast/betting operators
- **Flag:** CONTROVERSIAL/NOTABLE — federal criminal ban on commercial greyhound racing. (Note: penalty clause references "paragraphs (1) through (5)" of (a) though (a) has only paragraphs (1)–(4) — apparent drafting inconsistency.)

### Sec. 12010 — Placement of animals used in Federal research

### Sec. 12111 — Amplifying Processing of Livestock in the United States (A-PLUS)
- **Does:** Directs Secretary (within 1 year) to revise 9 C.F.R. 201.67 to allow market agencies to own/finance/manage a packer if the packer is below capacity thresholds: cattle/sheep < 2,000/day or < 700,000/year; hogs < 10,000/day or < 3,000,000/year. Requires disclosure of such ownership/financial relationships to livestock sellers. Savings clause preserves Packers & Stockyards authority.
- **Amends:** Directs revision of regulation (9 C.F.R. 201.67); under Packers and Stockyards Act, 1921 (7 U.S.C. 181 et seq.)
- **Numbers:** Cattle/sheep cap: 2,000/day or 700,000/yr; hogs: 10,000/day or 3,000,000/yr
- **Dates:** Within 1 year of enactment
- **Type:** REFORM
- **Affected:** Small/regional packers; livestock market agencies/auctions
- **Flag:** Loosens long-standing conflict-of-interest rule for small packers

### Sec. 12114 — Pilot program to support custom slaughter establishments

- **Flag:** Creates a dedicated counterintelligence-style ag-land enforcement chief

## Cross-cutting flags for this title
- **Animal disease / biosecurity:** NAHLN, NADPRP, and the National Animal Vaccine and Veterinary Countermeasures Bank (incl. FMD vaccine bank) reauthorized FY2027–2031; admin cap raised 10%→15% (Sec. 12001). Foreign-animal-disease export pre-negotiation authority (12004) and producer-support preparedness report (12007). USDA Office of Homeland Security gets biennial food/ag critical-infrastructure risk assessments and IC detailees (12201).
- **Proposition 12 / interstate-commerce preemption:** Sec. 12006 is a major EATS-Act-style federal preemption barring states from imposing production standards on out-of-state livestock-derived products — but it **excludes eggs** (covers slaughter livestock and dairy). High-controversy item.
- **Foreign ownership of US ag land (the title's center of gravity):** A coordinated package — AFIDA penalty/transparency overhaul + CFIUS info-sharing + public naming of penalized filers (12301); annual reports on foreign-country-of-concern purchases (12302); a new SES "Chief of Operations of Investigative Actions" counterintelligence office (12303); a foreign-ownership database with repeal of the 2023 appropriations-act provision (12304); USDA seated on CFIUS with adversary-deal referral and per-country sunsets (12305); and an outright presidential-directed ban on purchases by foreign adversaries/terror sponsors (12306).
- **Animal welfare:** Federal greyhound-racing ban (12008, effective 10/1/2027, up to 7 years' imprisonment); animal-fighting gambling ban (12009); research-animal retirement/adoption mandate (12010); dental-exam addition to "adequate veterinary care" + pet-protection report (12406); live-dog import regime moved from AWA to AHPA (12005).
- **Beginning / socially-disadvantaged / underserved & women farmers:** Commission on Farm Transitions—Needs for 2050 revived and broadened to heirs' property, succession, and barriers for historically underserved and women farmers (12401); biennial land-access reports with program catalog including heirs'-property title clearing (12404); Office of Partnerships and Public Engagement reauthorized to 2031 (12202); NAD burden-of-proof shifted onto the agency, aiding producer-appellants (12203).
- **Small/local meat & poultry:** A-PLUS market-agency/packer ownership liberalization with capacity caps (12111); small/very-small HACCP guidance (12112); cooperative interstate shipment outreach (12113); custom-slaughter direct-sale pilot sunset 9/30/2031 (12114).
- **Seafood expansion:** New USDA Office of Seafood (12419) and extension of FSA farm loans to commercial fishing/fish processing (12420) — a significant redefinition of "farmer."
- **Studies/reports (numerous):** Cattle fever tick (12002), FAD producer support (12007), Texas water (12408), Arizona water (12417), Rio Grande Valley working group (12412), organic-program barriers (12414), precision-ag satellite/FCC (12418), plant-detention transparency (12405), personnel/drought-monitor reauths (12402/12403). Multiple Water-Treaty-1944 items reflect a Texas/Arizona water-delivery focus.
- **Environmental/regulatory carve-outs (controversial):** Forest biomass declared carbon-neutral (≤0 GHG/carbon intensity) for USDA purposes (12409); farm equipment exempted from Clean Air Act emission standards (12422).
- **Honey:** Two anti-fraud sections — harmonized federal honey definition (12416) and a US-located-lab testing mandate barring sole reliance on foreign certificates (12421).
- **Grain Standards (Subtitle E):** USGSA reauthorized to FY2033, recurring "fund"→"trust fund" relabeling, "official agencies" added to several authorities, technology-adoption emphasis, and a discretionary report converted to a mandatory annual (Dec. 1) report (12501–12510).
- **Drafting note:** Sec. 12008(c) penalty clause references "paragraphs (1) through (5)" of subsection (a), but subsection (a) only contains paragraphs (1)–(4) — apparent cross-reference error.
