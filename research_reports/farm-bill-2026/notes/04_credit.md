# Title V — Credit — Notes

## Title-level summary
Title V reauthorizes and substantially expands USDA Farm Service Agency (FSA) farm credit programs through FY2031 and makes a sweeping set of changes to the Consolidated Farm and Rural Development Act (ConActR / CONACT, 7 U.S.C. 1921 et seq.) and the Farm Credit Act of 1971 (12 U.S.C. 2001 et seq.). The headline changes are large increases to FSA loan limits: direct farm ownership rises from $600,000 to $850,000 and guaranteed farm ownership from $1,750,000 to $3,500,000; direct operating rises from $400,000 to $750,000 and guaranteed operating from $1,750,000 to $3,000,000; and the microloan cap doubles from $50,000 to $100,000. It eases eligibility (shifting "a majority" ownership tests to "at least a 50 percent," and accommodating qualified operators, operating-only entities, and embedded entities), shortens the farm-management experience requirement from 3 years to 2, and creates new tools including guaranteed-to-direct loan refinancing, an expedited approval pilot, and heirs'-property legal-services cooperative agreements ($60M/yr FY2027–2031). It authorizes Farm Credit System (FCS) institutions to finance essential rural community facilities (capped at 15% of outstanding loans) and makes several FCS regulatory and lending-authority changes. A large technical-corrections section (Sec. 5508) modernizes agency references, removes obsolete county-committee roles, and adjusts inventory-property and interest-rate provisions. Most reauthorizations extend sunset dates from 2023 to 2031.

## Section-by-section

### Sec. 5101 — Persons eligible for real estate loans
- **Does:** Loosens owner-operator eligibility for direct farm ownership loans. Changes ownership test from "a majority" to "at least a 50 percent." Replaces subparagraphs (A)/(B) of §302(a)(2): (A) deems "qualified operators" (as defined by the Secretary) to meet the operator requirement; (B) allows an operating-only applicant to qualify if one or more owners of the farm real estate owns at least 50% (or other % Secretary sets) of the applicant; (C) deems an embedded entity (or chain of entities) to meet the direct-ownership requirement if at least 75% of total ownership interests is owned, directly or indirectly, by qualified operators.
- **Amends:** §302(a) of the Consolidated Farm and Rural Development Act (7 U.S.C. 1922(a)).
- **Numbers:** "at least a 50 percent" (replaces "a majority"); 50% owner threshold for operating-only entities; 75% qualified-operator threshold for embedded entities.
- **Dates:** —
- **Type:** REFORM
- **Affected:** Winners — operating-only entities, entities with complex/embedded ownership structures, qualified operators seeking direct farm ownership loans.
- **Flag:** Broadens access for entity/operator structures previously excluded by stricter ownership tests.

### Sec. 5102 — Experience requirements
- **Does:** Reduces the farm-management experience requirement for farm ownership loans. Strikes "3 years" → "2 years" in the eligibility matter; in the alternative-experience provisions strikes "3-year" → "2-year" and "1 or 2 years" → "1 year"; adds "or operational" before "responsibilities"; and adds a new catch-all alternative criterion clause (ix): "met any other criteria established by the Secretary."
- **Amends:** §302(b) of the Consolidated Farm and Rural Development Act (7 U.S.C. 1922(b)).
- **Numbers:** Experience requirement: 3 years → 2 years.
- **Dates:** —
- **Type:** DECREASE (lowers barrier) / REFORM
- **Affected:** Winners — beginning farmers and newer operators (less experience needed to qualify); Secretary gains discretion to add alternative criteria.
- **Flag:** —

### Sec. 5103 — Refinancing of indebtedness into direct loans
- **Does:** Adds new §303(d) directing the Secretary (acting through FSA Administrator), within 1 year of enactment, to issue regulations allowing certain FSA-guaranteed loans to be refinanced into FSA direct loans. Eligibility limited to loans that are: distressed/nonperforming without positive cash flow at lender's rates/terms; borrower in monetary default and subject to liquidation/foreclosure; with a reasonable chance of operational success; and meeting other taxpayer-protective criteria. Allows cross-program refinancing (guaranteed under one program into direct under another). Resulting direct loans remain subject to the maximum direct-loan limits (incl. §§305 and 313).
- **Amends:** §303 of the Consolidated Farm and Rural Development Act (7 U.S.C. 1923) — adds new subsection (d).
- **Numbers:** Subject to §305/§313 direct-loan caps (i.e., $850,000 FO / $750,000 OL post-this-title).
- **Dates:** Regulations within 1 year of enactment.
- **Type:** NEW
- **Affected:** Winners — distressed guaranteed-loan borrowers facing foreclosure who can be salvaged; FSA gains a workout tool.
- **Flag:** New guaranteed-to-direct conversion authority; rulemaking deadline.

### Sec. 5104 — Conservation loan and loan guarantee program
- **Does:** Adds new beneficiary category to conservation loan priorities: producers using loans to adopt precision agriculture practices or acquire precision agriculture technologies, including for participation in EQIP. Reauthorizes the conservation loan and loan guarantee program.
- **Amends:** §304 of the Consolidated Farm and Rural Development Act (7 U.S.C. 1924) — subsection (d) (adds paragraph (4)) and subsection (h) (extends sunset).
- **Numbers:** —
- **Dates:** Authorization extended from 2023 → 2031.
- **Type:** EXTEND / REFORM
- **Affected:** Winners — producers adopting precision agriculture and conservation/EQIP participants.
- **Flag:** —

### Sec. 5105 — Limitations on amount of farm ownership loans
- **Does:** Raises maximum farm ownership (FO) loan amounts. Direct FO loan ceiling: $600,000 → $850,000. Guaranteed FO loan ceiling: $1,750,000 → $3,500,000. Resets the inflation-adjustment base year from 2019 to 2026.
- **Amends:** §305(a)(2) of the Consolidated Farm and Rural Development Act (7 U.S.C. 1925(a)(2)).
- **Numbers:** Direct FO: $600,000 → **$850,000**. Guaranteed FO: $1,750,000 → **$3,500,000** (guaranteed amount doubles). Inflation indexing begins FY2026 (was FY2019).
- **Dates:** Inflation adjustment "beginning with fiscal year 2026."
- **Type:** INCREASE
- **Affected:** Winners — farm purchasers, especially in high-land-value areas; larger operations. Guaranteed lenders (banks/FCS) can write larger guaranteed FO loans.
- **Flag:** Headline number — guaranteed FO limit doubles to $3.5M.

### Sec. 5106 — Inflation percentage
- **Does:** Changes the index used to inflation-adjust farm ownership loan limits. Replaces the "Prices Paid By Farmers Index" (12-month period ending July 31) basis with a new basis: the equally-weighted average of per-acre U.S. farm real estate value, per-acre U.S. cropland value, and per-acre U.S. pasture value (from NASS's Agricultural Land Values report), comparing the preceding year to the year immediately preceding it.
- **Amends:** §305(c) of the Consolidated Farm and Rural Development Act (7 U.S.C. 1925(c)) — paragraphs (1) and (2).
- **Numbers:** Three components weighted equally (real estate, cropland, pasture per-acre values).
- **Dates:** —
- **Type:** REFORM
- **Affected:** Ties FO loan-limit escalation to land-value appreciation rather than input prices; tends to track real-estate inflation. Affects all future FO borrowers via adjusted caps.
- **Flag:** Indexing methodology change could materially affect future limit growth.

### Sec. 5107 — Authority of Farm Credit System institutions to provide financial support for essential rural community facilities projects
- **Does:** Adds new §4.18B to the Farm Credit Act of 1971, authorizing Farm Credit Banks, direct lender associations, and banks for cooperatives to make/participate in loans and provide technical and financial assistance for essential community facilities in rural areas eligible for financing under §306(a) of the ConActR. Only §306(a)-eligible entities may receive assistance. Limits: aggregate financing/assistance under this section may not exceed 15% of the institution's total outstanding loans; institution must first offer an interest in the financing (on reasonable terms) to at least one non-FCS domestic lending institution (other than USDA) and report the offer to the Farm Credit Administration (FCA), giving priority to community banks in the facility's service area. Requires an annual FCA report to House and Senate Agriculture Committees (also posted on FCA website).
- **Amends:** Farm Credit Act of 1971 — inserts new §4.18B after §4.18A (12 U.S.C. 2206a). References §306(a) ConActR.
- **Numbers:** Aggregate cap = **15% of total outstanding loans** of the institution. Offer to ≥1 non-FCS lender required.
- **Dates:** Effective October 1, 2026. First FCA report within 1 year of enactment, then annual.
- **Type:** NEW
- **Affected:** Winners — FCS institutions (new lending authority); rural communities needing essential community facilities. Mixed — commercial/community banks (offered participation but face new FCS competition); community banks get priority on offered participations.
- **Flag:** Notable expansion of FCS lending scope into rural community facilities; community-bank offer/priority requirement is a guardrail.

### Sec. 5108 — Down payment loan program
- **Does:** Adjusts the down payment loan program cap calculation. Changes "exceed 45 percent of the least" to "exceed, subject to section 305(a), 45 percent of the lesser"; conforms conjunctions in subparagraphs (A)/(B); and strikes subparagraph (C) (removing one of the prior cap-comparison prongs).
- **Amends:** §310E(b)(1) of the Consolidated Farm and Rural Development Act (7 U.S.C. 1935(b)(1)).
- **Numbers:** 45% cap retained; now subject to §305(a) FO limit; reduces comparison from three prongs to two ("lesser" of two).
- **Dates:** —
- **Type:** REFORM
- **Affected:** Beginning and socially disadvantaged farmers using the down payment program (links cap to the raised §305(a) FO limit).
- **Flag:** —

### Sec. 5109 — Heirs property
- **Does:** (a) Reauthorizes the heirs' property intermediary relending program (2023 → 2031). (b) Adds new §509 to Title V of the Rural Development Act of 1972, directing the Secretary to enter cooperative agreements with eligible nonprofit entities to provide legal/accounting services at no cost to "underserved heirs" to resolve undivided (fractional/multiple-owner) ownership interests on farmland/forest land — to transition land into or maintain agricultural production and to improve access to USDA programs. Agreements run up to 4 years (extendable if ownership unresolved); require annual progress reports; allow termination for failure to demonstrate success; permit limited service to non-underserved heirs in defined circumstances. Defines "eligible entity," "limited resource heir," and "underserved heir" (limited resource heir; member of a socially disadvantaged group per §2501(a) of the 1990 FACT Act; or a veteran per 38 U.S.C. 101(2)). Requires annual reports to House/Senate Agriculture Committees. Authorizes $60,000,000 per fiscal year for FY2027–2031. (c) Amends §310I(f) to require the relending-program operations/outcomes report annually (rather than once, 1 year after enactment).
- **Amends:** §310I(g) (7 U.S.C. 1936c(g)); §310I(f) (7 U.S.C. 1936c(f)); and Title V of the Rural Development Act of 1972 (7 U.S.C. 2661–2669) — new §509.
- **Numbers:** Authorization **$60,000,000/yr FY2027–2031**; cooperative agreements ≤4 years; limited-resource thresholds (income at/below national poverty level for family of 4, or <50% of county median household income over prior 2 years).
- **Dates:** Relending program reauthorized 2023 → 2031; appropriations FY2027–2031; annual congressional reports.
- **Type:** NEW (cooperative-agreement program) / EXTEND (relending) / STUDY-OR-REPORT (annual reports)
- **Affected:** Winners — underserved heirs (socially disadvantaged groups, veterans, limited-resource heirs) with fractional/heirs' property; nonprofit legal/accounting service providers.
- **Flag:** Significant equity-oriented provision with dedicated $60M/yr funding; targets the long-standing heirs'-property barrier to USDA program access.

### Sec. 5110 — Prompt approval of loans and loan guarantees
- **Does:** Replaces §333A(g)(1) with new provisions: requires the Secretary to provide lenders a short, simplified application form for real estate and operating guaranteed loans of not more than $1,000,000; requires notice of approval/disapproval within 5 business days of a complete application from a Preferred Certified Lender or Certified Lender; and caps the maximum guarantee percentage on such loans at 90% (loans ≤$125,000), 75% (>$125,000 and ≤$500,000), or 50% (>$500,000 and ≤$1,000,000). Redesignates old (2)/(3) as (3)/(4) and inserts new paragraph (2) requiring an accelerated application process for Business & Industry (B&I) guaranteed loans to rural entities (§310B(a)(2)(A)) up to $400,000, extendable to $600,000 if no significant increased default risk. Strikes subsection (h).
- **Amends:** §333A of the Consolidated Farm and Rural Development Act (7 U.S.C. 1983a).
- **Numbers:** Simplified form threshold ≤$1,000,000; 5-business-day notice; guarantee caps 90%/75%/50% tied to $125,000 / $500,000 / $1,000,000 tiers; B&I accelerated process ≤$400,000 (up to $600,000 if low risk).
- **Dates:** —
- **Type:** REFORM (process streamlining)
- **Affected:** Winners — Preferred/Certified Lenders and their borrowers (faster decisions); rural B&I loan applicants. Note: the new tiered guarantee caps could *reduce* the maximum guarantee % on larger streamlined loans versus standard guarantees.
- **Flag:** Guarantee-percentage ceilings (esp. 50% on the largest tier) are a notable tradeoff for the expedited track.

### Sec. 5111 — Expedited approval pilot program

Directs the Secretary to consider streamlining §339(c)(5) certifications/assessments and §360(b) determinations. Cannot waive substantive requirements other than application-timing requirements. Requires annual report to House/Senate Agriculture Committees. Authority terminates September 30, 2031. Conforming amendments to §346(b)(2) insert "to the extent practicable" qualifiers on certain timing deadlines (April 1 and September 1 of the fiscal year).
- **Amends:** Subtitle D of the ConActR — new §333E (after §333D, 7 U.S.C. 1983d); conforming amendments to §346(b)(2) (7 U.S.C. 1994(b)(2)).
- **Numbers:** —
- **Dates:** Pilot begins within 1 year of enactment; terminates September 30, 2031; annual reports.
- **Type:** NEW (pilot) / STUDY-OR-REPORT
- **Affected:** Winners — creditworthy direct/guaranteed FO borrowers and Preferred Certified Lenders.
- **Flag:** Sunsets 9/30/2031.

### Sec. 5508 — Technical corrections
- **Does:** Extensive technical/conforming corrections across the ConActR and Farm Credit Act. Highlights:
  - (a) Eliminates obsolete county-committee references (§§333A, 336, 339, 359); removes county-committee certification of borrower eligibility (FSA staff/borrower-meeting standard instead).
  - (b) Revises loan-assessment requirements (§360(d)(1)) to "periodic review (as determined by the Secretary) of direct and guaranteed loans" (drops mandatory annual review of direct loans).
  - (c) Updates outdated agency references throughout (Farmers Home Administration / Rural Development Administration / Rural Electrification Administration → Farm Service Agency, Rural Development, Rural Utilities Service) across many sections.
  - (d) Amends §335(c)(1) inventory-property timelines: subparagraph (A) "15" → "60"; (B)(i) "135" → "180" and adds "suitable for farming and ranching, as determined by the Secretary"; (C) revises disposition timing (e.g., not later than 60 days after the 180-day period for property not suitable for farming/ranching).
  - (e) Corrects "infeasible inventory property disposition framework" (§§331(b)(1), 335(f)) — strikes certain paragraphs, redesignates, and requires the Secretary to issue regulations ensuring release of funds to each borrower.
  - (f) Replaces "district office" with "District Director" (§333A(a)(2)(B)).
  - (g) Replaces obsolete "Trust Territory of the Pacific Islands" with "the Federated States of Micronesia, the Republic of Palau, and the Republic of the Marshall Islands" (§343(a)(6)).
  - (h) Revises farmer-program-loan definition (§343(a)(10)) to fold in conservation loans (CL under §304) on/after June 18, 2008.
  - (i) Eliminates inconsistency for beginning farmers (§343(a)(11)(C)) — "related to one another by blood or marriage" → "qualified beginning farmers."
  - (j) Updates provisions to reflect repurposing of conservation loan provisions (§§303(a), 310D).
  - (k) Updates notice method (§353(i)(1)) — "registered or certified mail" → "any method that provides documentation of delivery."
  - (l) "Soil Conservation Service" → "Natural Resources Conservation Service" (§306(a)(13)).
  - (m) Clarifies interest-rate requirements (§§307(a)(3)(B), 316(a)(2)): rate "be equal to the interest rate for direct farm ownership loans under this subtitle, not to exceed 5 percent per year."
  - (n) Corrects a heading (§309(h)(6)).
  - (o)–(t) Various deletions of superfluous restrictions, confusing guarantee references, obsolete reporting requirements (§346 subsections (c)/(d) struck), obsolete appeals provisions (replacing §333B references with National Appeals Division / reorganization-act citations), removal of a pilot-project constraint (§333D(a)), and a Farmer Mac heading fix (§8.8(a)(3) "Mortgage loans" → "Loan quality").
- **Amends:** Numerous sections of the Consolidated Farm and Rural Development Act (7 U.S.C. 1921 et seq.) and the Farm Credit Act of 1971.
- **Numbers:** Inventory timelines 15→60 days, 135→180 days, 60 days after 180-day period; interest-rate cap not to exceed 5% per year.
- **Dates:** June 18, 2008 reference (conservation loan definition).
- **Type:** REFORM (technical/conforming corrections; some substantive)
- **Affected:** FSA borrowers (inventory disposition, interest rates, notice methods); modernizes agency nomenclature. Note (b) and (d)/(e) are substantively meaningful, not purely cosmetic.
- **Flag:** Despite the "technical corrections" label, subsections (b), (d), (e), (m) carry real operational effects (loan reviews, inventory-property disposition, interest-rate parity/cap).

### Sec. 5509 — Report on improving creditworthiness of direct and guaranteed loan borrowers
- **Does:** Requires the Secretary, within 1 year of enactment, to report to House/Senate Agriculture Committees evaluating the feasibility of requiring adoption of certain risk-management practices as a condition for approving certain direct/guaranteed farm loans. Practices to evaluate: hedging/marketing price-or-revenue risk strategies; insurance optimization/coordination; periodic financial reporting/management; cash-management services; integrated monitoring/analytics tools; other risk mitigation; and voluntary lender incentives. Also evaluate feasibility of a certification program identifying loans approved contingent on enhanced risk-management practices. Applies only to operating, production, and working-capital loans for agricultural production — not real estate, rural development, housing, B&I, or other non-operating programs.
- **Amends:** New freestanding provision (report requirement; not codified).
- **Numbers:** —
- **Dates:** Report within 1 year of enactment.
- **Type:** STUDY-OR-REPORT
- **Affected:** Potentially operating/production-loan borrowers (future conditions); lenders (voluntary incentives). Study-only at this stage.
- **Flag:** Foreshadows possible future risk-management conditions on operating loans.

### Sec. 5510 — Farm Credit Administration option to examine low-risk Farm Credit System institutions on a 24-month cycle
- **Does:** Amends FCA examination cadence (§5.19(a)): changes "in no event" to "not," and authorizes FCA, in its sole discretion, to extend the time between mandatory examinations of institutions it deems small, low-risk to not more than 24 months.
- **Amends:** Farm Credit Act of 1971 §5.19(a) (12 U.S.C. 2254(a)).
- **Numbers:** Mandatory-exam interval extendable to **not more than 24 months** for small, low-risk institutions.
- **Dates:** Effective October 1, 2026.
- **Type:** REFORM
- **Affected:** Winners — small, low-risk FCS institutions (reduced exam frequency); FCA gains discretion.
- **Flag:** Lengthened examination cycle is a deregulatory/relief measure for small institutions.

## Cross-cutting flags for this title
- **Loan-limit increases are the marquee changes.** Direct FO $600,000→$850,000; guaranteed FO $1,750,000→$3,500,000 (Sec. 5105); direct OL $400,000→$750,000; guaranteed OL $1,750,000→$3,000,000 (Sec. 5202); microloan $50,000→$100,000 (Sec. 5203). Inflation indexing rebased from FY2019 to FY2026, and the index switched from input prices to land values (Sec. 5106) — likely faster future growth in FO caps.
- **Eligibility liberalization is systematic.** Across FO (5101), OL (5201), and EM (5301) loans, "a majority" ownership test becomes "at least a 50 percent," and new "qualified operator," "operating-only entity" (≥50% owner), and "embedded entity" (≥75% qualified-operator-owned) rules broaden access for complex farm business structures.
- **Beginning / socially disadvantaged / underserved farmer support:** shorter 2-year experience requirement (5102); reauthorized IDA pilot (5401), loan set-asides (5403), down payment program tied to higher FO cap (5108); and a new, funded heirs'-property legal-services program ($60M/yr FY2027–2031) targeting socially disadvantaged groups, veterans, and limited-resource heirs (5109).
- **Farm Credit System expansions and protections:** new authority for essential rural community facilities capped at 15% of outstanding loans (5107); aquaculture-services eligibility (5501); rural water/waste/telecom/electricity financing (5503); export-finance limit rebased to 15% of total assets (5502); FCA established as "sole and independent regulator" with express-override clause (5504); Farmer Mac single-borrower limit set at 10% of tier 1 capital (5506); and a 24-month exam option for small, low-risk institutions (5510).
- **Process streamlining with tradeoffs:** guaranteed-to-direct refinancing (5103), simplified applications and 5-business-day decisions but tiered guarantee caps of 90%/75%/50% (5110), and an expedited approval pilot sunsetting 9/30/2031 (5111).
- **Reauthorizations consistently extend sunsets from 2023 → 2031** (5104, 5109(a), 5204, 5401, 5402, 5403, 5404, 5507(c)).
- **"Technical corrections" (5508) contains substantive items** — dropping mandatory annual direct-loan review, revised inventory-property disposition timelines (15→60, 135→180 days), and a 5% interest-rate cap parity provision — beyond pure nomenclature updates.
- **Two reports/studies:** Sec. 5509 (risk-management loan conditions feasibility study, operating loans only) and recurring annual reports (5107, 5109, 5111).
- **Effective-date clustering on October 1, 2026** for FCS provisions (5107, 5510); Sec. 5504 effective at enactment.
