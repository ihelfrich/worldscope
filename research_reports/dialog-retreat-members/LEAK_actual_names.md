# The *actual* Dialog leak — full names released (June 2026)

*This documents the **real, WIRED-verified leak**, not the satirical video. Names
+ public titles only. The sensitive per-person fields that were also exposed
(political-leaning scores, contact tokens, matchmaking/questionnaire answers,
private attendance "gradings") are **deliberately excluded**.*

## What actually happened

- **Dialog** is a real, invitation-only, off-the-record society **co-founded in
  2006 by Peter Thiel and Auren Hoffman**; executive director **Raffi Grinberg**.
- In **June 2026**, Swiss hacktivist **maia arson crimew** (who previously exposed
  the US "No Fly List"), acting on an anonymous tip, found that **dialog.org**
  exposed an **unprotected directory embedded in the website's source code**.
- **WIRED** (with **The Hollywood Reporter**) first reported it and said it
  **independently verified** the documents' authenticity.
- **Two datasets leaked:**
  1. a **"Directory List" of 113 people** — crimew extracted and published this on
     Bluesky ("all 113 alleged members we were able to successfully extract").
     Later reporting stressed it **mixes members with past speakers and guests**.
  2. a **2026 retreat registration roster of ~222 people** — retreat planned for
     **Aug 12–16, 2026, near Dublin, Ireland**; 87 listed as first-time attendees.
- The **113 list is reproduced in full below** (the canonical roster). For the
  222-registrant file, only the marquee names were published by outlets; those are
  listed in the second section. **Being named ≠ confirmed "membership"** — several
  people disputed involvement (flagged).

> **The satire was built directly on this real leak.** Nearly every name in the
> @thomasvangenderen video maps to the 113 list — confirming the video dramatized
> a genuine breach rather than inventing one.

## Caveats
- **"Named ≠ member."** The 113 directory conflated members, guests, and past
  speakers; crimew said so explicitly. Himes, Polis, Kallas (and Brolin in the
  registration file) had involvement questioned or denied.
- **Private fields excluded by choice.** The dump also held political-leaning
  scores, contact tokens, and matchmaking answers Dialog promised to keep private;
  not reproduced here.
- **One source error corrected:** trendingtopics.eu transposed the titles of
  **Mario Schlosser** (Oscar Health co-founder) and **Will Scharf** (White House
  Staff Secretary); fixed above.
- **Spelling flags:** Nicolas (not "Nicholas") Berggruen; "Gaurva"→Gaurav Kapadia;
  Jon = Jonathan Levin.
