### Caroline Cochran (overlay: "Caroline Cochra—") — HIGH confidence (Oklo, not Oxide)
- **Identification:** Caroline Cochran (Caroline Cochran DeWitte), **Co-Founder & COO
  of Oklo Inc.** (advanced fission / SMRs; SPAC merger May 2024). The transcript's
  "Oxide Computer" guess is **incorrect** — the record points to Oklo.
- **Founded:** Co-founded **Oklo** (2013) with **Jacob (Jake) DeWitte** (CEO, her
  co-founder and spouse).
- **Notable associates:** Jacob DeWitte; **Sam Altman** (former Oklo Board Chair,
  stepped down April 2025); investors include **Peter Thiel**, Dustin Moskovitz, Ron
  Conway (SV Angel), Tim Draper.
- **Connective tissue:** **Strong and direct.** Oklo received VC from **Hydrazine
  Capital** (founded by **Sam Altman**, with **Peter Thiel as its sole limited
  partner**); Altman chaired Oklo's board; Thiel held a notable post-IPO stake. This
  places Cochran firmly within the Altman/Thiel AI-and-energy investment orbit — the
  strongest Thiel-tie among the uncertain reads.
