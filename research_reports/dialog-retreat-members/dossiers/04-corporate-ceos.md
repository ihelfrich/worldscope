# Dossier 04 — Corporate CEOs / founders

*Public-record OSINT. "Dialog" membership is the video's satirical/unverified
claim. Items marked "(unverified)" lack a confirming primary source.*

### Neal Mohan
- **Primary role (2026):** CEO of YouTube (Alphabet); *Time* CEO of the Year 2025. Led YouTube since Feb 2023.
- **Career:** Career executive at Google/YouTube (and earlier DoubleClick, Microsoft); holds Alphabet equity. Not a founder.
- **Board seats:** Starbucks board; Paley Center for Media trustee; Stanford GSB Advisory Council; **Council on Foreign Relations** member. Former boards: Stitch Fix (resigned Dec 2023), 23andMe (resigned Sept 2024).
- **Notable associates:** Sundar Pichai (his direct superior); shares CFR membership with **Vas Narasimhan**.
- **Connective tissue:** Runs one of the world's largest AI-driven recommendation platforms inside Alphabet. No direct Thiel-orbit tie; former 23andMe seat sat adjacent to the genomics-data sector.

### Vas Narasimhan
- **Primary role (2026):** CEO of Novartis (since 2018).
- **Career:** Career Novartis executive (former global head of drug development, CMO); holds Novartis equity. Not a founder.
- **Board seats:** **Appointed to the board of Anthropic (April 2026)** — reported as the first healthcare professional on that board, via a trust arrangement. PhRMA board (former chair); chair of African Parks; **CFR** member.
- **Notable associates:** Through Anthropic, connected to its leadership (Dario Amodei et al.); shares CFR with Neal Mohan; PhRMA peers.
- **Connective tissue:** The strongest direct AI tie in this batch — his 2026 Anthropic board seat places him in the frontier-AI orbit (pharma–AI convergence). No direct Thiel relationship; Anthropic is not Thiel-founded.

### Demet Mutlu
- **Primary role (2026):** Founder and CEO of Trendyol Group, Turkey's largest e-commerce "superapp" (majority-owned by **Alibaba** since 2018). Founded Trendyol in 2009.
- **Firms:** Founder/CEO of Trendyol (and verticals Trendyol Go, Trendyol Pay, Dolap). Co-founder/investor in Peak Games (sold to Zynga 2020 for ~$1.8B).
- **Board seats:** Leads Trendyol's board; WEF community member.
- **Notable associates:** Alibaba leadership (majority shareholder); Peak Games co-founders. No named overlap with others in this dossier (unverified).
- **Connective tissue:** A consumer-tech/e-commerce founder with a data-rich platform but the most peripheral to the Thiel/AI orbit — ties run to Alibaba and the Turkish/European tech ecosystem.

### Mario Schlosser
- **Primary role (2026):** Co-Founder and Advisor to the CEO of Oscar Health (effective June 1, 2026), focused on AI/digital-health; remains on Oscar's board. (Previously President of Technology / CTO; earlier co-founder and CEO.)
- **Firms:** Co-founded **Oscar Health** (2012) with **Joshua Kushner** and Kevin Nazemi; holds vested Oscar equity. Earlier co-founded Vostu (LatAm social gaming).
- **Notable associates:** **Joshua Kushner** (Oscar co-founder; founder of **Thrive Capital**) and Kevin Nazemi (all three HBS classmates).
- **Connective tissue:** The clearest Thiel-orbit link in this batch — Oscar Health was backed by **Founders Fund (Thiel's firm)**, which led a $165M round in 2018 (led by FF partner Brian Singerman). Early backers also Khosla Ventures, General Catalyst, 8VC, and Kushner's Thrive Capital.

### Scott Cook
- **Primary role (2026):** Founder and Chairman of the Executive Committee of Intuit (TurboTax, QuickBooks, Credit Karma, Mailchimp). Co-founded Intuit in 1983. (CEO is Sasan Goodarzi.)
- **Firms:** Co-founder of Intuit; major Intuit shareholder (billionaire). Operates the Valhalla Foundation.
- **Board seats:** Intuit board; former long-time board member of eBay and Procter & Gamble.
- **Notable associates:** Sasan Goodarzi; Brad Smith (former Intuit CEO). No named overlap with others in this dossier (unverified).
- **Connective tissue:** A foundational fintech/SaaS founder; Intuit is a major AI adopter in consumer finance. No direct Thiel relationship; Valley-establishment standing places him adjacent to, not inside, the Thiel network (unverified).

### Mike Cannon-Brookes
- **Primary role (2026):** Co-founder and (since Sept 2024) **sole CEO** of Atlassian, after Scott Farquhar stepped down as co-CEO.
- **Firms:** Co-founded Atlassian (2002, with Scott Farquhar); major shareholder. Founder of **Grok Ventures** (family office / VC).
- **Investments / board seats:** Atlassian board. Via Grok Ventures: Canva, Culture Amp, **SpaceX**, Brighte, SunDrive Solar, Fleet Space, Goterra, and the Sun Cable solar megaproject. High-profile climate activist investor (former AGL Energy stake).
- **Notable associates:** Scott Farquhar; Annie Cannon-Brookes (Grok co-principal).
- **Connective tissue:** Enterprise-software founder + deep-tech investor; the notable orbit overlap is **SpaceX** via Grok Ventures (a portfolio investment, not a personal Thiel relationship). Network is more climate-tech / Australian than Thiel-aligned.

### Stan McChrystal
- **Primary role (2026):** Founder, CEO and Chairman of McChrystal Group (leadership consulting, founded 2011). Retired four-star U.S. Army general; former ISAF Afghanistan and JSOC commander.
- **Firms:** Founder/owner of McChrystal Group; author and speaker.
- **Board seats:** Board chair, Service Year Alliance; senior fellow, Yale Jackson Institute. Corporate/board service reported at Deutsche Bank America, JetBlue, Navistar, Siemens Government Technologies, **FiscalNote**, Accent Technologies (current/past mix — some may be former, unverified).
- **Connective tissue:** National-security/defense-leadership figure. Siemens Government Technologies and defense-adjacent boards put him in the government/defense-tech sphere that intersects **Palantir** thematically (no direct Palantir or Thiel relationship identified). FiscalNote (govtech/data) is the closest data-sector tie.

### Peggy Hamburg (Margaret Hamburg)
- **Primary role (2026):** Public-health leader; 21st FDA Commissioner (2009–2015). Active in scientific-governance/advisory roles.
- **Board seats:** Director, **Alnylam Pharmaceuticals**; chair of the board, AAAS; co-chair, InterAcademy Partnership; **Vice Chair of the President's Intelligence Advisory Board**; Foreign Affairs Policy Board; Foreign Secretary, National Academy of Medicine; Global Board, The Nature Conservancy. Former: Rockefeller Foundation, Rockefeller University, Henry Schein.
- **Notable associates:** Pharma/biotech and public-health governance figures; sector overlaps **Vas Narasimhan** (no confirmed personal tie, unverified).
- **Connective tissue:** Bridges biomedicine, regulation, and national-security/intelligence advisory (PIAB) — the intelligence-advisory role is the most notable data/national-security touchpoint. No direct Thiel relationship (unverified).
