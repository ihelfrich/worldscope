### Peter Thiel
- **Primary role (2026):** Co-founder / General Partner, Founders Fund; Chairman of Palantir Technologies. Estimated net worth ~$27–29B, anchored by Palantir equity (unverified exact figure).
- **Companies founded / owned / controlled:** PayPal (co-founder, ex-CEO — "don" of the PayPal Mafia); Palantir Technologies (2003/04, co-founder, board chairman, ~4% stake per 2026 SEC filing); Founders Fund (2005, co-founder, ~42.5% of the management entity); Clarium Capital (founder, macro hedge fund); first outside investor in Facebook (2004).
- **Major investments / board seats / fund affiliations:** Founders Fund raised a record ~$6B fourth growth fund in May 2026 (unverified final size); 2026 deals include Onodrim (defense), Arda (AI), and a personally-led round in Panthalassa (ocean data centers); early backer of OpenAI (no longer actively involved); broad PayPal-Mafia portfolio exposure (Anduril, Stripe, SpaceX).
- **Notable professional associates:** Elon Musk (PayPal co-founder); Reid Hoffman (PayPal founding team); Joe Lonsdale (protégé — Stanford Review, PayPal, Clarium, Palantir co-founder); Alex Karp (Palantir CEO); Ken Howery, Luke Nosek (Founders Fund co-founders). Greg Brockman, Reid Hoffman, and Charlie Songhurst reportedly co-appear with Thiel on the leaked "Dialog" roster (unverified).
- **Connective tissue:** The literal hub — co-founder of both PayPal (the Mafia) and Palantir, mentor to Lonsdale, early OpenAI investor, and reported co-founder/host of the "Dialog" network (with Auren Hoffman, ~2006).

### Elon Musk
- **Primary role (2026):** CEO and largest shareholder of Tesla and SpaceX; reported to have become the first US-dollar trillionaire in mid-2026 (unverified). SpaceX reportedly completed an IPO in June 2026 (~$1.77T valuation; unverified).
- **Companies founded / owned / controlled:** SpaceX (2002); Tesla (CEO since 2008, largest shareholder); xAI (reportedly folded into SpaceX as its AI division in 2026, unverified); X/Twitter (acquired 2022); Neuralink (2016); The Boring Company (2016); X.com (merged into PayPal).
- **Major investments / board seats:** Operator-owner rather than a fund VC; no significant passive board seats outside his own companies.
- **Notable professional associates:** Peter Thiel; Reid Hoffman; Greg Brockman and Sam Altman (OpenAI co-founders, now litigation adversaries); Jason Kwon (OpenAI CSO, opposing him).
- **Connective tissue:** Co-founded OpenAI in 2015 with Altman, Brockman et al. In *Musk v. Altman*, a California jury reportedly dismissed all his claims (May 2026) on statute-of-limitations grounds; appeal signaled (unverified). PayPal lineage ties him to the Thiel/Hoffman Mafia; OpenAI co-founding ties him to Brockman and Kwon.

- **Companies founded / owned / controlled:** Palantir Technologies (2004, with Thiel, Karp, Cohen, Gettings; left day-to-day 2009, remains shareholder); Addepar; OpenGov; Affinity; Epirus (directed-energy/counter-drone defense); 8VC-built companies including Saronic (autonomous maritime) and Resilience (biomanufacturing); Cicero Institute.
