# Dossier 05 — Academics / authors / public intellectuals

claim. Items marked "(unverified)" lack a confirming primary source.*

> **not** editor-in-chief of *The Free Press* — that publication is Bari Weiss's;
> Stephens edits *SAPIR*. (2) The "Rick Warren" of evangelical ministry is almost

### Tyler Cowen
- **Primary role (2026):** Holbert L. Harris Chair of Economics, George Mason University; chairman/general director of the Mercatus Center at GMU.
- **Affiliations:** GMU; Mercatus; columnist for *The Free Press* (since April 2025, after ~8 years at Bloomberg Opinion); co-author of the Marginal Revolution blog (with Alex Tabarrok); host of "Conversations with Tyler."
- **Founded/advised:** Founder of **Emergent Ventures** (2018) at Mercatus; co-founder of Marginal Revolution University.
- **Notable associates:** Alex Tabarrok; **Peter Thiel** (recurring podcast guest; the **Thiel Foundation funded Emergent Ventures' launch** — a $1M grant). Podcast guests include dossier members Ezra Klein and Jonathan Haidt.
- **Connective tissue:** Strong, documented Thiel/tech ties via the Thiel-Foundation grant and Thiel's early podcast appearance.

### Adam Grant
- **Primary role (2026):** Saul P. Steinberg Professor of Management and Professor of Psychology, Wharton (UPenn); organizational psychologist, bestselling author, podcast host.
- **Affiliations:** Wharton; co-director of Wharton People Analytics; host of TED's "Re:Thinking"/"WorkLife"; co-host of "The Curiosity Shop" with Brené Brown.
- **Founded/advised:** Curator of the Next Big Idea Club (with Susan Cain, Malcolm Gladwell, Daniel Pink). Corporate org-psych consulting (current board seats unverified).
- **Notable associates:** Susan Cain, Malcolm Gladwell, Daniel Pink, Brené Brown; Robert Cialdini (documented intellectual engagement). Next Big Idea Club has platformed Haidt and Klein.
- **Connective tissue:** No documented direct Thiel business relationship (unverified); intra-dossier links via Cialdini, Haidt, Klein.

### Susan Athey
- **Primary role (2026):** The Economics of Technology Professor, Stanford GSB; a pioneering "tech economist."
- **Affiliations:** Stanford GSB; Senior Fellow at SIEPR and Stanford HAI; Founding Director of the Golub Capital Social Impact Lab; NBER. Past President of the American Economic Association (2023). Former Chief Economist, U.S. DOJ Antitrust (2022); former consulting chief economist for Microsoft.
- **Board seats:** Have included Expedia, LendingClub, Rover, Turo, and **Ripple**; Innovations for Poverty Action; scientific advisor at Haus. (Current 2026 directorships unverified.)
- **Notable associates:** **Jon Levin** (Stanford GSB colleague and Stanford President).
- **Connective tissue:** Deeply embedded in the tech/AI economics orbit (Ripple, Microsoft, Stanford HAI). Reports of an OpenAI nonprofit advisory role unconfirmed (unverified).

### Jonathan Haidt
- **Primary role (2026):** Social psychologist; Thomas Cooley Professor of Ethical Leadership, NYU Stern; bestselling author.
- **Affiliations:** NYU Stern (since 2011); "After Babel" Substack (with Zach Rausch); previously University of Virginia.
- **Founded/advised:** Co-founder of Heterodox Academy (board chair Jan 2018–Aug 2025); the "Anxious Generation"/"After Babel" initiatives.
- **Notable associates:** Tyler Cowen (repeat podcast guest); Greg Lukianoff (*Coddling* co-author); Adam Grant and Bret Stephens (fellow "Dialog" roster names).
- **Connective tissue:** His thesis targets Big Tech / social media. No documented direct Thiel relationship (unverified).

### Ezra Klein
- **Primary role (2026):** NYT opinion columnist (since 2021) and host of "The Ezra Klein Show."
- **Affiliations:** The New York Times (Opinion); co-founded **Vox Media** (2014, with Matthew Yglesias and Melissa Bell); earlier WaPo, The American Prospect. Associated with the "abundance" movement.
- **Notable associates:** Derek Thompson (*Abundance* co-author); Tyler Cowen (podcast guest, 2025); Matthew Yglesias, Melissa Bell; collegial adjacency to Bret Stephens (NYT Opinion).
- **Connective tissue:** Tech/AI orbit mainly through subject matter (*Abundance*; AGI discussion); appeared on Lex Fridman. No documented direct Thiel connection (unverified).

### Sam Harris
- **Primary role (2026):** Neuroscientist/philosopher/author; host of "Making Sense"; founder of the Waking Up meditation app.
- **Affiliations:** Operates independently via Sam Harris Media / Waking Up LLC. Ph.D. neuroscience, UCLA. 2026 Richard Dawkins Award.
- **Founded:** Waking Up (2018); co-founder of the nonprofit Project Reason.
- **Notable associates:** Bret Stephens (repeat guest); **Eric Weinstein** (managing director of **Thiel Capital**; frequent podcast interlocutor); Richard Dawkins, Ben Shapiro.
- **Connective tissue:** Closest Thiel link is via Eric Weinstein (Thiel Capital), a recurring guest. Prominent commentator on AI existential risk. No documented direct relationship with Thiel himself (unverified).

### Robert Cialdini
- **Primary role (2026):** Social psychologist; President/CEO of Influence at Work; co-founder of the Cialdini Institute. Regents' Professor Emeritus, Arizona State.
- **Affiliations:** Arizona State (emeritus); historical visiting roles at Stanford and UCSC.
- **Founded:** Influence at Work; Cialdini Institute (~2022, with CEO Bas Wouters). Author of *Influence* and *Pre-Suasion*.
- **Notable associates:** Bas Wouters; Adam Grant (intellectual engagement).
- **Connective tissue:** Primary dossier link is to Adam Grant. No documented connection to the Thiel/tech/AI orbit — the clearest outlier among the academics.

### Kim Scott
- **Primary role (2026):** Author and co-founder/CEO of Radical Candor; executive coach. Radical Candor recently launched "Compass," an AI coaching tool.
- **Affiliations:** Former faculty at Apple University; former leader at Google (AdSense, YouTube, DoubleClick); Princeton alumna.
- **Founded:** Radical Candor LLC; Just Work, LLC (with Trier Bryant). Advisor to Twitter, Qualtrics, Shyp; earlier founded Juice Software and was CEO of Delta Three.
- **Notable associates:** **Sheryl Sandberg** (former Google boss, central to Radical Candor's origin); Trier Bryant; Brett Berson / First Round Capital.
- **Connective tissue:** Strong tech-world ties via Google, Apple, and SV VC, plus a current generative-AI product. No documented connection to Thiel specifically (unverified).

### Bret Stephens
- **Primary role (2026):** Opinion columnist, The New York Times (since 2017); editor-in-chief of **SAPIR** (since 2021); senior contributor to NBC News. Pulitzer winner (2013); former WSJ deputy editorial page editor; former editor-in-chief of The Jerusalem Post.
- **Affiliations:** NYT; NBC News; SAPIR (published by the Maimonides Fund). *(Correction: he is not EIC of The Free Press — that is Bari Weiss's.)*
- **Notable associates:** **Bari Weiss** (both left WSJ around the same period, later at NYT); Sam Harris ("Making Sense" guest); Ezra Klein (NYT Opinion colleague).
- **Connective tissue:** Thiel/tech links are indirect — via Bari Weiss and The Free Press orbit (Thiel gave Weiss a high-profile post-2024-election interview). No documented direct Thiel business relationship.

### Jon Levin
- **Primary role (2026):** 13th President of Stanford University (since Aug 2024); economist (IO, market design, antitrust).
- **Affiliations:** Stanford (President; previously Dean of Stanford GSB 2016–2024); Stanford Digital Economy Lab; NBER; Aspen Economic Strategy Group; WEF.
- **Board seats:** American Century Funds; Trustee, Gordon and Betty Moore Foundation; former PCAST (2021–2025); consulted for Google, eBay, Comcast, Yahoo!, Zynga, FCC, U.S. Treasury.
- **Notable associates:** **Susan Athey** (Stanford GSB economics-of-technology colleague).
- **Connective tissue:** Tech-economics consulting (Google, eBay) and Digital Economy Lab role tie him to the tech/AI orbit; direct GSB-economist link to Athey.

### Nick Thompson
- **Primary role (2026):** CEO of The Atlantic (since Feb 2021); journalist and author.
- **Affiliations:** The Atlantic (CEO); formerly EIC of **WIRED** (~2017–2021) and editor of The New Yorker's website; speaker at Aspen Ideas and the ITU "AI for Good" Summit; National Committee on American Foreign Policy. (Aspen board membership unverified.)
- **Notable associates:** Intra-dossier adjacency to Ezra Klein, Sam Harris, Jon Levin, Susan Athey (co-listed names).
- **Connective tissue:** As former WIRED EIC and Atlantic CEO, deeply embedded in tech journalism and AI discourse (recurring AI-summit speaker).

### Rick Warren
- **Primary role (2026):** Founding/emeritus pastor of Saddleback Church (stepped down as senior pastor 2022; succeeded by Andy Wood); Executive Director of the "Finishing the Task" missions coalition; bestselling author (*The Purpose Driven Life*, 50M+ copies).
- **Affiliations:** Saddleback Church (founder, emeritus); "Finishing the Task"; Daily Hope broadcast ministry.
- **Connective tissue:** No documented links to the Thiel/tech/AI orbit or to the other people in this dossier — the clear outlier, with an evangelical-ministry rather than tech/academic/media network.
