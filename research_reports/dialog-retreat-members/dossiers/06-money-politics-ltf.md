## Leading the Future (LTF) super PAC

**What it is.** A real, well-documented pro-AI super PAC network launched **August
2025** to push AI-industry-friendly policy — chiefly a single federal regulatory
framework in place of state-by-state AI laws — and to reward/punish candidates in
the 2026 midterms. Co-led by **Josh Vlasto** (former Fairshake spokesman) and **Zac
Moffatt** (CEO of GOP consulting firm Targeted Victory). Explicitly modeled on the
crypto industry's **Fairshake** PAC. **(REAL)**

**Who funds it (verified):**
- **Andreessen Horowitz (a16z)** — reported $50M+. **(REAL)**
- **Greg Brockman** — OpenAI president/co-founder, a launch backer (LTF is *not*
  OpenAI the company). **(REAL)**
- **Joe Lonsdale** — Palantir co-founder. **(REAL)**
- Also reported: **Perplexity**, VC **Ron Conway**, and other industry stakeholders.
- Affiliated/offshoot entities: **Think Big** (Dem-facing affiliate), **American
  Mission** (GOP-facing), and **Build American AI**, a 501(c)(4) advocacy arm
  reportedly led by Nathan Leamer. **(REAL)**

**Reported size.** The **"$125 million" figure is REAL** — LTF and affiliates raised
~$125M in H2 2025 (incl. the ~$100M launch); entered 2026 with ~$70M cash on hand.
Some later coverage cites "over $140M" cumulatively. **(REAL)**

**The "$10M against Alex Bores" figure.** **REAL but figures vary.** Bores publicly
said Silicon Valley was spending "$10 million" against him; post-primary reporting
put actual outside spending at roughly **$8 million**. **(REAL; ~$8–10M)**

**Races/candidates actually targeted or backed (verified):**
- **Opposed: Alex Bores** (NY-12 Dem primary) — LTF's first major target, via Think
  Big. **Bores LOST the June 24, 2026 primary** to **Micah Lasher** (who also
  co-sponsored the RAISE Act). **(REAL)**
- **Supported (Democrats):** reported endorsements include **Ritchie Torres (NY-15)**,
  **Rob Menendez (NJ-8)**, **Val Hoyle (OR-4)** (May 2026 tranche); and an earlier set
  naming **Josh Gottheimer (NJ)**, **Sam Liccardo (CA)**, **Jimmy Gomez (CA-34)**,
  **Yvette Clarke (NY-9)**, **Suhas Subramanyam (VA-10)**. For Torres, LTF reportedly
  spent **$982,000+** via Think Big. **(REAL; amounts vary)**
- **Supported (Republicans):** e.g. **Chris Gober (TX)**; broader GOP-primary spending
  across TX, GA, IL, MT. **(REAL)**
- Also entered **state legislative races**. **(REAL)**

**Controversies (reported):** AI-generated fake "journalist"/sockpuppet accounts;
paying influencers to frame Chinese AI as a security threat; shell-company structures
critics say evade transparency. The Trump White House was reportedly "irked." **(REAL)**

## aimoneywatch.org

**What it is.** A real watchdog tracker using **FEC filings** to follow LTF spending —
who it funds, who it targets, how much. Sections: `/people`, `/races`, `/ltf`. **(REAL)**

**Who runs it.** A project of **Demand Progress / Demand Progress Action**, the
progressive advocacy group; launch announced by Demand Progress with **Colin McGlynn**
as AI policy adviser; methodology page published. **(REAL)**
