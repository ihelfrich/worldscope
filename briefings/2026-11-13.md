## Macro situation

The US rate structure is in an unusual configuration. The effective fed funds rate stands at [3.88%](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/DFF) as of 11 November, SOFR at [3.89%](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/SOFR), the 2-year at [4.73%](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/DGS2), the 10-year at [4.98%](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/DGS10) and the 30-year at [5.35%](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/DGS30). The policy rate sits 1.10 percentage points below the long bond and 85 basis points below the 2-year. In a normal tightening cycle the front end leads the Fed by 25 to 50 basis points. An 85 basis point gap says the market expects not one hike but a sequence [high].

![US rate structure: policy rate sits well below the long end](briefings/2026-11-13-yield_curve.png)

That reading is confirmed directly. Polymarket prices a 25 basis point December hike at [88%, on $1.01 million of 24-hour volume](https://polymarket.com/), with "no change" at 2% and a 50 basis point move at 9%. A [Reuters poll of 101 economists conducted 6 to 10 November](https://finance.yahoo.com/economy/policy/articles/fed-raise-rates-once-more-124512933.html) forecast a quarter-point increase to a 4.00% to 4.25% range at the 9 December meeting. Governor **Christopher Waller** [said he leans toward a quarter point but could back a half-point move](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/10/fed-governor-waller-sees-december-hike-likely.html) if inflation data deteriorates, speaking on 10 November at the Bank of England Watchers Conference in London. Dallas Fed President **Lorie Logan** has backed at least two more quarter-point increases.

The driver is an October CPI print that [rose 0.4% headline and 0.3% core](https://www.americanbanker.com/news/hot-inflation-report-puts-fed-on-track-for-december-rate-hike). The index level reached [337.476](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/CPIAUCSL) with core at [339.900](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/CPILFESL). Reporting attributes the persistence to supply-side shocks, tariffs and the war in Iran, rather than to demand. That distinction matters for how the hiking cycle ends: a Fed tightening against supply shocks is choosing to break demand to offset a relative price change, which is the configuration that historically produces policy mistakes in both directions [medium].

The labor side gives the Fed room. Unemployment sits at [4.1%](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/UNRATE), nonfarm payrolls at [159.293 million](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/PAYEMS), and JOLTS job openings at [7.523 million](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/JTSJOL) as of September. An openings-to-unemployed ratio in that neighborhood is consistent with a labor market that is tight but no longer overheating. Waller's 10 November speech was titled ["The Economic Outlook and Some Thoughts on Labor Market Dynamism"](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/waller20261110a.htm), and the choice of subject by the committee's most vocal December hawk suggests the hawks are building a case that labor slack is not accumulating fast enough to stay their hand [medium].

The 10y-2y spread at [+0.24](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/T10Y2Y) is positive but thin. The historical significance of a spread this narrow during an active hiking cycle is that it leaves almost no cushion: two more quarter-point moves at the front end, with the long end anchored, would invert the curve by the February meeting. Curve inversions have preceded every US recession since 1955 with one false positive in the mid-1960s, which is why a +0.24 reading with a hiking Fed deserves more attention than the same number in a cutting cycle [high].

## Cyber & biosecurity

**First-of-kind KEV callout.** On 9 November CISA added [CVE-2026-102895 in GPUStack](https://nvd.nist.gov/vuln/detail/CVE-2026-102895) to the Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog with a three-day federal remediation deadline of 12 November. GPUStack is an open-source GPU cluster manager used to serve large language models and other AI workloads across pooled accelerators, and this is the first model-serving orchestration platform of its kind to appear on the catalog. The structural signal is that AI inference infrastructure has crossed from emerging technology into the category of software that CISA tracks as actively exploited against federal networks, which means the attack surface being defended now includes the control plane for GPU fleets rather than only the applications running on them.

The mechanism deserves precision because it generalizes. Before version 2.2.0, GPUStack treated any request originating from loopback as the built-in administrator whenever `force_auth_localhost` was false, which was the default. [A same-host reverse proxy triggers this by default, and an attacker can forge an `X-GPUStack-Real-IP` header to the same effect](https://blog.ostorlab.co/gpustack-auth-bypass-rce-cve-2026-102895.html). [The vendor reported attacks against a 2.1.1 deployment in which attackers created inference backends that ran reverse shells](https://labs.cloudsecurityalliance.org/research/csa-research-note-gpustack-cve-2026-102895-kev-20261110-csa/), and an attacker could also mint API keys and retrieve secrets. Trusting a header for identity is a decades-old mistake, and the fact that it reappeared in AI serving infrastructure suggests this software generation is repeating the web stack's early security history at compressed speed [medium]. Remediation is upgrade to 2.2.0 or later.

The rest of the KEV additions run routine but concentrated. [CISA added four flaws on 9 November with a 12 November deadline](https://www.esecurityplanet.com/vulnerabilities/news-cisa-kev-gpustack-freeswitch-openssh-ibm-november-2026/): GPUStack, SignalWire FreeSWITCH code injection, OpenBSD OpenSSH, and IBM Aspera Faspex. A second batch landed 10 November with a 24 November deadline, covering Microsoft Office Outlook, Openwave Webtop Media Transcoder, Cisco BroadWorks, Google Chromium use-after-free, and two Barracuda products whose deadline was today. Two Ivanti Connect Secure CVEs from 30 October remain, one of which also carried a deadline of today. Fifteen KEV entries at -0.3 sigma is a normal week by volume, but three separate network-edge vendors (Barracuda, SonicWall, Ivanti) in a single fortnight is a concentration worth watching [medium].

Tech security reporting adds three live campaigns: [hackers exploiting N-central flaws to infect 21 servers](https://thehackernews.com/), a [worm-like "SCKit" malware targeting managed service providers through ScreenConnect](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/), and [4,800 compromised GitHub accounts used to steal cloud credentials](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/).
