The Iran deal clock expired without a formal agreement

Polymarket's US–Iran permanent peace deal by May 31 prints at 4%, down from 8% a week ago; the Hormuz blockade-lifting contract fell from 33% to 18% in 48 hours. In the same window Ukraine's SBU launched its most significant deep-strike package in weeks — drones destroyed two Tu-142 maritime patrol aircraft and an Iskander system at Taganrog airfield and struck the Feodosiya oil terminal in Crimea. Russia retaliated overnight with 290 drones and five Kh-101 cruise missiles; air defences claimed 284 kills. Crimea began fuel rationing at 20 litres per person per day this morning.
